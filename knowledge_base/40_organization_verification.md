1. ORGANIZATION VERIFICATION — OVERVIEW 
Organization Verification is the process through which an organization establishes and 
verifies its business identity on WiseIN. 
WiseIN's current Organization information states that organizations are verified using 
official business information and documents, including CIN, GST, PAN, and official 
business registration documents. WiseIN describes this verification as part of its effort to 
maintain an authentic professional network and reduce fake organizations and identity 
fraud.  
The current WiseIN Organization offering specifically lists: 
• 
CIN Verification — India  
• 
Global Organization Verification — Sumsub  
• 
Verified Organization Badge  
• 
Individual Organization Profile.   
The Organization plan also currently lists CIN & GST verification for the company as part 
of the plan features.  
 
2. PURPOSE OF ORGANIZATION VERIFICATION 
Organization Verification helps WiseIN establish that an organization is a legitimate 
business or eligible organization before providing its verified organization presence. 
The verification process is intended to support: 
• 
Authentic organization identities  
• 
Trust between businesses  
• 
Verified company profiles  

• 
Reduced fake organizations  
• 
Safer professional networking  
• 
More reliable hiring interactions  
• 
Verified organization-to-organization collaboration  
• 
Verified employee/company representation.  
WiseIN describes its Organization platform as a network where companies are verified 
using official company information and documentation.  
 
3. ORGANIZATION VERIFICATION VS INDIVIDUAL VERIFICATION 
These are separate verification concepts. 
Individual Verification 
Verifies an individual person's identity. 
WiseIN's public website describes individual verification around government-issued 
identity information such as Aadhaar, DigiLocker, and PAN.  
Organization Verification 
Verifies the organization's business identity using organization/company information and 
applicable business documents. 
WiseIN's Organization documentation references: 
• 
CIN  
• 
GST  
• 
PAN  
• 
Official business registration documents.   
Important chatbot rule 
Do not tell a user that completing individual Aadhaar verification automatically verifies 
their organization. 
Likewise, organization verification should not be described as a replacement for individual 
identity verification.

29. PENDING VERIFICATION 
If an organization is shown as pending, the safest explanation is: 
Your organization verification is still being processed. WiseIN's Organization information 
states that verification can take approximately 24–48 hours after the required 
information/documents are submitted. 
If the user has already exceeded the stated timeframe, the chatbot can recommend 
checking the current status and contacting WiseIN support. 
Do not assume the reason for the delay.

31. INFORMATION MATCHING 
Organization verification depends on accurate information. 
The user should enter organization information that corresponds with the organization's 
official records. 
For example: 
• 
Organization name should correspond to the official business identity.  
• 
CIN should be the organization's correct CIN.  
• 
Organization email should be an email controlled by the organization.  
• 
Verification documents should belong to the organization being registered.  
Do not instruct users to enter someone else's CIN or use an unrelated organization's 
information.

42. VERIFICATION TROUBLESHOOTING — EMAIL 
Problem: Organization email verification does not work 
Recommended steps: 
1. Confirm the organization email is entered correctly.  
2. Select Verify.  
3. Follow the verification instructions shown by WiseIN.  
4. Check the organization's email inbox and spam/junk folders if WiseIN sends an 
email.  
5. Do not share the verification code with anyone.  
6. Retry if the UI provides a retry/resend option.  
7. Contact WiseIN support if the issue persists.  
Do not invent an exact sender address or expiry period. 

 
43. VERIFICATION TROUBLESHOOTING — CIN 
Problem: CIN verification fails 
Recommended steps: 
1. Confirm that the CIN belongs to the organization being created.  
2. Check the entered CIN carefully.  
3. Make sure the organization information is accurate.  
4. Use the Verify action shown in the current UI.  
5. Follow any error message shown by WiseIN.  
6. If the problem continues, contact WiseIN support.  
Do not guess why a particular CIN was rejected.

61. GOLDEN ANSWERS 
Q: What is Organization Verification? 
Organization Verification is WiseIN's process for confirming an organization's business 
identity using official company information and applicable business documents. WiseIN 
currently references CIN, GST, PAN, and official business registration documents for 
organization verification. 
Q: What is required to create an organization? 
The current WiseIN creation screen shows required fields for Organization Name, 
Organization Email, Organization CIN, Organization Logo, and Organization URL Name. The 
Bio field is optional and currently shows a 100-character limit. 
Q: Is Organization Email verification required? 
Yes. The current Organization creation screen marks Organization Email as required and 
provides a Verify button. 
Q: Is CIN required? 

Yes. The current WiseIN Organization creation screen marks Organization CIN as required 
and provides a Verify button. 
Q: What is the organization bio limit? 
The current WiseIN creation UI shows a 100-character limit for the organization Bio. 
Q: What logo formats are supported? 
The current UI shows PNG and JPG, with an 800×400 pixel specification. It also says larger 
logos can be cropped. 
Q: What is Organization URL Name? 
Organization URL Name is the required name used as part of your organization's WiseIN 
URL. The current UI displays the structure wisein.in/Organization/ followed by the URL 
name you enter. 
Q: How does WiseIN verify organizations? 
WiseIN's current Organization information states that organizations are verified using CIN, 
GST, PAN, and official business registration documents. For India, WiseIN specifically lists 
CIN Verification. Global Organization Verification is currently listed through Sumsub. 
Q: How long does organization verification take? 
WiseIN currently states that organization verification can take approximately 24–48 hours 
after the required information/documents are submitted. Actual processing time can vary. 
Q: What happens after organization verification? 
Once the applicable verification process is completed, the organization can receive a 
Verified Organization Badge and use WiseIN's organization features according to its 
account and plan. 
Q: Is a verified organization guaranteed to be trustworthy? 
Verification confirms the organization's WiseIN verification status. It should not be 
interpreted as a guarantee of business quality, financial performance, employment 
outcomes, or other business characteristics. 
Q: Is organization verification the same as individual verification? 
No. Organization verification establishes the organization's business identity, while 
individual verification establishes a person's identity. They are separate verification 
processes.
