25. Meetings 
✦ Overview 
The Meetings feature on WiseIN allows users to participate in professional online meetings and 
communicate with other members through scheduled or available meeting sessions. 
Meetings are separate from regular Chat/Messaging. While Chat is primarily used for 
text-based communication, Meetings provide a space for direct, real-time interaction. 
 
25.1 Accessing Meetings 
Users can access Meetings from the main WiseIN navigation. 
Navigation 
WiseIN → Meetings 
The Meetings section is available from the left-side navigation menu. 
 
25.2 Purpose of Meetings 
Meetings can be used for professional interactions such as: 
●​ Networking 
●​ Professional discussions 
●​ Career conversations 
●​ Business discussions 
●​ Collaboration 
●​ Knowledge sharing 
●​ Discussions with other WiseIN members 
The exact meeting capabilities should follow the functionality currently available in the WiseIN 
application.

25.7 Meeting Privacy 
Meetings can involve private conversations and professional information. 

Users should respect the privacy of other participants. 
According to WiseIN's Content Policies: 
●​ Meeting conversations should not be publicly shared without appropriate permission. 
●​ Meeting recordings should not be published without explicit permission. 
●​ Meeting transcripts should not be publicly shared without appropriate consent. 
●​ AI-generated meeting summaries should not be publicly shared without the other 
participant's consent. 
 
25.8 Meeting Recordings 
WiseIN's Content Policies specifically address meeting recordings. 
If a meeting is recorded: 
Participants should know that the meeting is being recorded. 
Users should not secretly record another person. 
Recorded meeting content should not be publicly shared without appropriate permission. 
 
25.9 AI Meeting Summaries 
WiseIN's Content Policies also refer to AI meeting summaries. 
An AI-generated meeting summary may contain information from the conversation. 
Users should treat meeting summaries as private unless appropriate permission has been 
obtained from the other participants. 
Important 
The AI Assistant should not tell users that they can publicly share an AI meeting summary 
unless WiseIN's current policy explicitly allows it. 
 

25.10 Meeting Recording Consent 
For recorded meetings, users should ensure that participants are aware of the recording. 
The WiseIN Content Policies state: 
If a call is being recorded, both people must know. 
Therefore, users should not secretly record another participant.

25.13 Meetings and Events 
WiseIN also provides an Events functionality. 
Meetings and Events should not automatically be treated as the same feature. 
Meeting 
A direct online interaction or scheduled meeting. 
Event 
A broader organized activity that may involve multiple participants. 
The AI Assistant should identify which feature the user is asking about before providing 
instructions. 
 
25.14 Meeting Troubleshooting 
Problem: Meeting does not open 
Try: 
1.​ Check the internet connection. 
2.​ Refresh WiseIN. 
3.​ Open the Meetings section again. 
4.​ Select the meeting again. 
5.​ Try joining again. 
If the issue continues, contact WiseIN Customer Support. 
 

Problem: Cannot join a meeting 
Check: 
●​ Whether the meeting is currently available. 
●​ Whether the user has access to the meeting. 
●​ Whether the meeting has already ended. 
●​ Whether the internet connection is working. 
If the problem continues, escalate to Customer Support. 
 
Problem: Video or audio is not working 
Basic troubleshooting: 
1.​ Check the device's internet connection. 
2.​ Check browser/device permissions. 
3.​ Confirm that the correct microphone/camera is available. 
4.​ Refresh the meeting. 
5.​ Rejoin the meeting. 
If the issue remains unresolved, contact WiseIN Support. 
Knowledge-base note: Specific browser/device troubleshooting steps should only 
be added after they are confirmed by WiseIN's technical documentation. 
 
25.15 Meeting Safety 
Users should be careful when sharing information during meetings. 
Avoid unnecessarily sharing: 
●​ Passwords 
●​ OTPs 
●​ Banking credentials 
●​ Authentication codes 
●​ Government ID information 
●​ Confidential company information 
●​ Other people's private information 

If another participant behaves inappropriately or attempts fraud, the user should report the issue 
through the available WiseIN reporting/support channels. 
 
25.16 Reporting Meeting Problems 
Users should contact WiseIN Support when they experience: 
●​ Meeting access problems 
●​ Repeated audio/video failures 
●​ Meeting crashes 
●​ Unexpected meeting termination 
●​ Recording problems 
●​ Privacy concerns 
●​ Suspicious participant behavior 
●​ Other unresolved meeting issues 
When reporting a technical issue, useful information includes: 
●​ Meeting name 
●​ Approximate date/time 
●​ Description of the issue 
●​ Steps that caused the problem 
●​ Error message, if any 
●​ Screenshot or screen recording, if available 
●​ Device/browser information 
Users should never provide passwords or OTPs to support. 
 
25.17 Common AI Assistant Questions 
Q1. Where can I find Meetings? 
Open the Meetings section from the WiseIN main navigation. 
Q2. How do I join a meeting? 
Open Meetings, select the relevant meeting, review the available meeting 
information, and use the available join option. 

Q3. Can I use Chat instead of a meeting? 
Yes. Chat is used for text-based communication, while Meetings are intended for 
real-time interaction. 
Q4. Can meetings be recorded? 
WiseIN's Content Policies address meeting recordings. If a meeting is recorded, 
participants must be aware of the recording. Secretly recording another participant 
is not allowed. 
Q5. Can I share a meeting recording? 
Meeting recordings should not be publicly shared without appropriate permission 
from the participants. 
Q6. Can I share an AI meeting summary? 
WiseIN's Content Policies state that AI meeting summaries are personal and should 
not be publicly shared without the other participant's consent. 
Q7. My meeting is not opening. What should I do? 
Check your internet connection, refresh WiseIN, and try opening the meeting again. 
If the problem continues, contact WiseIN Customer Support. 
Q8. My camera or microphone isn't working. 
Check your device and browser permissions, verify your camera and microphone, 
and try rejoining the meeting. If the issue continues, contact WiseIN Support.
