70. FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions 
Overview 
The FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions) section provides quick answers to common questions 
users may have about WiseIN. 
This section is intended to help users get immediate answers without needing to contact 
customer support for every basic question. The AI customer-support assistant can also use 
these FAQs as a quick-reference layer alongside the detailed WiseIN knowledge base. 
 
70.1 General WiseIN Questions 
What is WiseIN? 
WiseIN is a professional networking platform designed to help users build authentic professional 
connections, communicate, discover opportunities, participate in communities, attend events, 
and interact with other verified members. 
 
Who can use WiseIN? 
Users must meet WiseIN's eligibility requirements. According to the Terms & Conditions, users 
must be at least 14 years old. 
 
Is WiseIN a professional networking platform? 
Yes. WiseIN is designed around professional connections, networking, communication, 
meetings, opportunities, and communities. 
 
Does WiseIN use verified profiles? 

Yes. WiseIN uses verification mechanisms to support authentic identities and trusted 
connections. 
 
70.2 Account & Profile 
How do I create a WiseIN account? 
Users can register through the WiseIN platform and complete the required account and profile 
information. 
 
Can I use a fake name? 
No. WiseIN's Terms & Conditions require users to use their real names and genuine profile 
information. 
 
Can I impersonate another person? 
No. Impersonation is prohibited. 
Users should not: 
●​ Pretend to be another person. 
●​ Pretend to represent an organization they do not work for. 
●​ Use another person's identity. 
●​ Use another person's photos or information deceptively. 
 
Can I create a business profile? 
Yes. Companies, organizations, teams, and communities can use dedicated business profiles. 
 
Can I delete my account? 
Yes. 

Go to: 
Settings → Delete Account 
The displayed Delete Account page states that deletion permanently removes the account and 
data and cannot be undone. 
 
Can I temporarily stop using WiseIN? 
Yes. 
Use: 
Settings → Deactivate 
Deactivation temporarily pauses the account and allows the user to reactivate it later. 
 
What is the difference between deactivation and deletion? 
Deactivation: Temporarily pauses the account and allows future reactivation. 
Deletion: Permanently removes the account and data and cannot be undone. 
 
How do I log out? 
Go to: 
Settings → Log Out 
WiseIN displays a confirmation popup asking: 
"Are you sure you want to logout from your account?" 
Select Logout to continue or Cancel to remain logged in. 
 
70.3 Login & Verification 

I cannot log in. What should I do? 
Try: 
1.​ Checking your login details. 
2.​ Checking your internet connection. 
3.​ Refreshing the page. 
4.​ Trying again. 
5.​ Trying another supported browser if necessary. 
If the problem continues, contact WiseIN support. 
 
Why am I getting "Failed to fetch" when using Google Sign-In? 
This error has been observed during WiseIN testing. 
Try: 
1.​ Checking your internet connection. 
2.​ Refreshing the page. 
3.​ Trying Google Sign-In again. 
4.​ Trying another browser. 
If it continues, report the error to WiseIN support. 
 
I didn't receive my OTP. What should I do? 
Try: 
1.​ Confirming that your phone number or email is correct. 
2.​ Checking your network connection. 
3.​ Waiting briefly. 
4.​ Requesting another OTP if available. 
5.​ Checking your spam/junk folder if the OTP is sent by email. 
If you still don't receive it, contact support. 
 
Why can't I save my education information? 

If the process requires OTP verification and the OTP is not received, you may be unable to 
complete the save operation. 
Try requesting another OTP and retrying the operation. If the problem continues, contact 
support. 
 
70.4 Content & Community 
What can I post on WiseIN? 
Examples of appropriate content include: 
●​ Things you are building or working on. 
●​ Things you have learned. 
●​ Questions. 
●​ Professional opportunities. 
●​ Events. 
●​ Achievements. 
●​ Honest experiences. 
●​ Useful knowledge. 
Content should be real, honest, useful, and appropriate for the WiseIN community. 
 
What content is not allowed? 
WiseIN's Content Policies prohibit various types of harmful or inappropriate content, including: 
●​ Violence 
●​ Hate speech 
●​ Discrimination 
●​ Sexually explicit material 
●​ Misinformation 
●​ False claims 
●​ Manipulated screenshots or conversations 
●​ Graphic or disturbing material 
●​ Impersonation 
●​ Fake credentials 
●​ Spam 
●​ Scams 
●​ Privacy violations 

●​ Copyright violations 
 
Can I promote my own work? 
Yes, provided the promotion is relevant and adds value. 
For example: 
Acceptable:​
 "I built this project and here's what I learned." 
The Content Policies specifically distinguish useful self-promotion from aggressive or repetitive 
promotional content. 
 
Can I post the same content repeatedly? 
No. Repetitive posts, copy-paste posts across multiple communities, and repeated messages 
can be considered spam. 
 
Can I share another person's private information? 
No. Users should not publicly share another person's: 
●​ Phone number 
●​ Email 
●​ Address 
●​ Personal details 
●​ Private conversations 
without appropriate permission. 
 
Can I post screenshots of private conversations? 
WiseIN's Content Policies state that private conversations, DMs, meeting chats, and private 
discussions should not be posted publicly without consent from everyone involved. 
 

70.5 Reporting & Safety 
How do I report a user? 
Open the relevant user's profile and use the available Report User option. 
The report form allows the user to select a reason, provide details, and optionally submit 
evidence. 
 
What can I report? 
The available reporting categories include: 
●​ Harassment 
●​ Spam 
●​ Inappropriate Content 
●​ Scam/Fraud 
●​ Impersonation 
●​ Violence 
●​ Other 
 
Can I upload evidence with a report? 
Yes. 
The WiseIN reporting interface allows: 
●​ Up to 3 images 
●​ Maximum 5 MB per image 
 
How long can my report details be? 
The report details field has a 500-character limit. 
 
Can I block someone? 
Yes. 

The Blocked section in Settings allows users to manage blocked users. 
 
What happens when I block someone? 
According to the WiseIN blocked-user interface, blocked users cannot: 
●​ Message you 
●​ Call you 
●​ See your activity 
Users can also unblock previously blocked users. 
 
70.6 Meetings & Video 
Can I have video meetings on WiseIN? 
Yes. WiseIN includes meeting and video-call functionality. 
 
Can I share a recording of a WiseIN meeting? 
WiseIN's Content Policies state that meeting recordings, transcripts, and AI summaries should 
not be shared publicly without the appropriate permission of the other participant. 
 
Can I secretly record a meeting? 
No. WiseIN's Content Policies state that participants must know if a call is being recorded. 
 
Can I share an AI meeting summary? 
The Content Policies state that AI meeting summaries are intended for the user and should not 
be publicly shared without the other person's consent. 
 

My video recording failed. What should I do? 
If you receive the "Some error!" message after submitting a recorded video: 
1.​ Check your internet connection. 
2.​ Check camera and microphone permissions. 
3.​ Try recording again. 
4.​ Refresh the page. 
5.​ Retry the submission. 
6.​ Try another browser if necessary. 
If the problem continues, report it to WiseIN support. 
 
70.7 Jobs & Opportunities 
Can I post a job on WiseIN? 
Yes, provided the opportunity meets WiseIN's requirements. 
 
What makes a job post acceptable? 
A job, internship, gig, or opportunity should be genuine. 
The poster should clearly communicate: 
●​ What the role is. 
●​ Who is offering it. 
●​ Compensation. 
●​ Whether it is paid or unpaid. 
●​ Whether it is part-time or full-time. 
●​ Other important conditions. 
 
Are scam job offers allowed? 
No. 
WiseIN's Content Policies prohibit fraudulent opportunities, including schemes such as: 

●​ Fake job offers. 
●​ Pay-first-to-get-a-job scams. 
●​ Pyramid schemes. 
●​ Multi-level marketing schemes. 
●​ Unrealistic "earn money quickly" offers. 
 
70.8 Subscriptions & Payments 
Does WiseIN have paid features? 
WiseIN may offer premium features, subscriptions, and additional paid services. 
 
What should I do if I have a payment problem? 
First check your payment/subscription status. 
If the issue remains unresolved, contact: 
support@wisein.in 
 
Are WiseIN payments refundable? 
The Terms & Conditions state that fees may be non-refundable except where required by law or 
under WiseIN's applicable refund policy.

70.10 AI Customer Support 
What should I do if the AI chatbot cannot solve my problem? 

The chatbot should offer human escalation when the issue cannot be resolved using the 
available WiseIN knowledge base or troubleshooting steps. 
 
Can I ask to speak with a human? 
Yes. 
Users can explicitly request human assistance when they need additional support. 
 
What happens during escalation? 
The chatbot should collect relevant information about the problem and route the issue to the 
appropriate human support channel. 
The conversation history and troubleshooting already attempted should be retained where the 
system supports it, so the user does not have to unnecessarily repeat the entire issue.

70.12 AI FAQ Rules 
For the WiseIN chatbot, FAQ answers should follow these principles: 
1. Answer directly 
Give the user the answer first instead of sending them through unnecessary steps. 
2. Use exact WiseIN terminology 
For example: 
●​ Deactivate Account 
●​ Delete Account 
●​ Report User 
●​ Blocked 
●​ Customer Support 
3. Do not invent information 
If a specific behavior is not documented, the AI should say that the available information does 
not specify it. 
4. Use the detailed knowledge base when necessary 

FAQ answers are for common questions. Complex issues should use the relevant detailed 
documentation. 
5. Escalate unresolved issues 
If the FAQ and troubleshooting information cannot solve the user's problem, the chatbot should 
move toward human escalation rather than repeatedly giving the same answer.
