1. OVERVIEW 
Communities are topic- or interest-oriented spaces within WiseIN where verified users 
can participate in focused professional discussions and share relevant content. 
WiseIN currently lists “Join Communities” as a feature of the Basic plan. Its Student plan 
separately lists “Campus Communities”, indicating that communities can also serve 
student-focused networking and campus-related use cases.  
WiseIN's Content Policies also explicitly refer to communities, community-specific rules, 
staying on topic, community creators, and community moderators. This confirms that 
communities are intended to have topic-specific participation and moderation.  
The public website does not, however, currently document every detail of the Community 
UI or backend workflow. Therefore, this document intentionally separates confirmed 
functionality from details that should not be invented by the chatbot.

3. COMMUNITIES AND WISEIN'S VERIFIED NETWORK 
Communities operate within WiseIN's broader verified professional-network environment. 
WiseIN describes itself as a professional network where members are verified through 
government identity verification, with the stated goal of creating a trusted environment for 
professional connections.  
This means the chatbot can describe Communities as part of WiseIN's broader 
professional networking ecosystem. 
However, avoid saying: 
“Every community member has completed a particular verification method.” 

unless the current verification requirements for community participation are confirmed by 
the live product. 
The general platform is verification-oriented, but community-specific access requirements 
are not fully documented publicly.

6. COMMUNITY-SPECIFIC RULES 
One of the most important documented aspects of WiseIN Communities is that individual 
communities may have their own rules. 
WiseIN's Content Policies state that each community may have its own content rules and 
give the example that an agriculture community can have different expectations from a 
startup community.  
Therefore: 
WiseIN global policies + individual community rules = applicable rules for community 
participation. 
A user should follow both. 
 
7. STAY ON TOPIC 

WiseIN explicitly requires users to stay on topic within a community. 
For example: 
If you join a design community, your posts should relate to design rather than unrelated 
subjects. 
WiseIN describes unrelated posting as hijacking a community and asks users to respect 
the purpose of the space.  
Chatbot answer 
Community posts should stay relevant to the community's topic and purpose. Each 
community may have additional rules that members are expected to follow.

9. COMMUNITY MODERATORS 
WiseIN's Content Policies explicitly mention community moderators.

11. COMMUNITY SPAM RULES 
WiseIN explicitly prohibits spam. 
The Content Policies specifically prohibit: 
• 
copy-paste posts across multiple communities  
• 
repetitive messages  
• 
repeatedly posting the same content to gain visibility.  
Therefore, the chatbot should advise users to avoid posting the same content repeatedly 
across communities. 
Recommended answer 
Avoid repetitive or copy-pasted posts across multiple communities. WiseIN's Content 
Policies prohibit spam and encourage users to make posts relevant and useful.  
 
12. SELF-PROMOTION IN COMMUNITIES 
Self-promotion is not automatically prohibited. 
WiseIN allows self-promotion when it is: 
• 
relevant  
• 
useful  
• 
value-adding  
The platform contrasts useful sharing of something you built or learned with aggressive 
promotional messages.  
Appropriate example 
“I built a small project using FastAPI. Here's what I learned about authentication.” 
Poor community behavior 
“BUY MY COURSE NOW!!! DM ME!!!” 

The chatbot should therefore distinguish relevant professional sharing from spam-like 
promotion. 
 
13. LINKS IN COMMUNITIES 
WiseIN specifically states: 
If you share a link, explain why it matters. 
Random or irrelevant links should not be dropped into communities, comments, or DMs.  
Recommended chatbot answer

Avoid dropping unrelated links into community discussions. 
 
14. JOBS AND OPPORTUNITIES IN COMMUNITIES 
Jobs and opportunities are allowed content on WiseIN, but they must comply with the 
platform's rules. 
WiseIN requires job, gig, internship, and opportunity posts to be genuine: 
•

• 
real company  
•

Users must also disclose whether an opportunity is: 
• 
unpaid

•

20. REPORTING MISUSE 
WiseIN also prohibits misuse of the reporting system.

•

•

• 
submit false reports.

21. COMMUNITY ENFORCEMENT 
WiseIN describes different levels of enforcement. 
For minor violations: 

1. First violation — content removed + warning  
2. Second violation — temporary feature restriction  
3. Third violation — account review  
For serious violations, WiseIN says immediate action may occur rather than following the 
three-step sequence. 
Severe violations can result in permanent bans.  
Chatbot guardrail 
Never promise: 
“Your report will definitely result in a ban.” 
The final enforcement decision belongs to WiseIN's moderation process. 
 
22. COMMUNITIES VS FEED 
Communities should not be treated as identical to the main WiseIN feed. 
Main Feed 
Broad platform-wide content and updates. 
Community 
Topic-specific discussion space with community-specific rules. 
The key distinction is: 
Feed = broader audience; Community = focused topic/community context. 
The exact feed/community UI relationship is not fully documented publicly, so the chatbot 
should not invent specific navigation or feed-filter behavior. 
 
23. COMMUNITIES VS POSTS 
A Post is a content item. 
A Community is a topic-oriented space where relevant content/discussions can take 
place. 
Therefore: 

“How do I create a post?” 
is a Posts question. 
“Which community should I join for this topic?” 
is a Communities question. 
“Why was my post removed from a community?” 
is both a Community + Content Policy question. 
 
24. COMMUNITIES VS EVENTS 
WiseIN separately lists public events and Student Networking Events as platform 
offerings.  
A community and an event should not be treated as the same entity. 
Community 
Ongoing topic-oriented space. 
Event 
A particular scheduled activity or gathering. 
A community may potentially discuss or promote an event, but the public documentation 
does not establish that every community has its own event system. 
 
25. COMMUNITIES VS AUDIO ROOMS 
WiseIN Premium currently advertises Audio Rooms Hosting, while Communities are listed 
separately as a platform feature.  
Therefore: 
• 
Community = topic/community space  
• 
Audio Room = live audio interaction feature

26. COMMUNITIES VS ORGANIZATIONS 

These are different concepts. 
Organization 
A company, institution, team, recruitment firm, university, incubator, or similar 
organizational presence on WiseIN. 
Community 
A topic- or interest-oriented space for members. 
WiseIN's current terms also distinguish companies, organizations, teams, and 
communities as different profile/context categories.  
Therefore, an Organization should not automatically be treated as a Community.

40. COMMON USER QUESTIONS 
“What are Communities on WiseIN?” 
Communities are topic- or interest-focused spaces where WiseIN members can 
participate in relevant discussions and share content. WiseIN supports joining 
Communities, and Student users have access to Campus Communities.  
“Can I join a community?” 
Yes. WiseIN currently lists “Join Communities” as a Basic feature. Student users also 
receive Campus Community access through the Student offering.  
“What are Campus Communities?” 
Campus Communities are the student-oriented community offering listed by WiseIN for 
Student users.  
“Can I post anything in a community?” 
Posts should be relevant to the community and must follow both the community's own 
rules and WiseIN's Content Policies.  
“Can I promote my project?” 
Yes, if the promotion is relevant and adds value. WiseIN allows relevant self-promotion but 
prohibits spam and repetitive promotional content.  
“Can I post the same content in multiple communities?” 
Avoid copy-pasting the same content across multiple communities. WiseIN's Content 
Policies prohibit this type of spam.  
“Can I post a job in a community?” 

Job and opportunity content must be genuine, and details such as unpaid, equity-only, or 
part-time status must be disclosed honestly. Scam and pay-to-get-a-job offers are 
prohibited.  
“Who moderates a community?” 
WiseIN's policies refer to community creators and community moderators. Community 
creators may set additional rules and remove content that does not fit, while moderators 
help enforce community standards.  
“Can community rules be different?” 
Yes. WiseIN explicitly states that individual communities may have their own content rules 
depending on their purpose.  
“Can I share someone's phone number in a community?” 
Not without their explicit permission. WiseIN prohibits publicly sharing another person's 
phone number, email, address, or personal details without permission.
