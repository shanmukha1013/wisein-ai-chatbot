1. EDUCATION — OVERVIEW 
Education is a section of the WiseIN individual profile where a user can present their 
academic background. 
The Education section helps users provide relevant academic information alongside other 
professional profile information such as: 
• 
About  
• 
Key Skills  
• 
Employment or experience  
• 
Projects  
• 
Website or professional links  
• 
Education  
• 
Profile video  
• 
Posts  
• 
Clips  
• 
Sessions  
• 
Webinars  
• 
Slots  
The current WiseIN profile interface includes an Education section with an “+ Add 
education” option. 
Education is part of the broader profile information that users may provide to enhance their 
WiseIN profile. WiseIN's Privacy Policy explicitly identifies education as profile information 
that users may choose to add.  

 
2. PURPOSE OF THE EDUCATION SECTION 
The Education section allows a user to communicate their academic background to other 
people viewing their WiseIN profile. 
Typical purposes include: 
• 
Showing current or previous academic institutions.  
• 
Providing academic context for a professional profile.  
• 
Helping other users understand a person's educational background.  
• 
Supporting professional networking.  
• 
Providing additional context when users discover people by skills, industry, and 
experience.  
• 
Helping students and fresh graduates present their academic background.  
WiseIN's public website describes discovery around individuals' skills, industry, and 
experience, while the Privacy Policy identifies education as part of profile information that 
users can provide.  
 
3. WHERE EDUCATION APPEARS 
Education appears as a section within an individual's WiseIN profile. 
In the current profile interface, the Education section appears below the main profile 
information and includes: 
Education 
+ Add education 
Existing education entries can be displayed in this section. 
The profile interface also provides an edit/action mechanism for profile sections and 
entries where applicable. 
 
4. ADDING EDUCATION 

When a user wants to add an academic qualification to their profile, they can use the “+ 
Add education” option in the Education section. 
General flow: 
1. Open the user's WiseIN profile.  
2. Locate the Education section.  
3. Select “+ Add education.”  
4. Enter the requested education information.  
5. Submit/save the education entry.  
6. The education entry becomes part of the user's profile.  
The exact form fields, validation rules, mandatory fields, date formats, dropdown options, 
and save behavior should be taken from the current authenticated WiseIN application. 
Chatbot rule: Do not invent field names or validation requirements that are not available in 
the current application or approved internal documentation. 
 
5. EDUCATION ENTRY 
An education entry represents one academic experience or qualification associated with 
the user's profile. 
A user may have more than one education entry if the current WiseIN interface allows 
multiple entries. 
Examples of academic history may include: 
• 
School education  
• 
Intermediate / higher secondary education  
• 
Diploma  
• 
Undergraduate degree  
• 
Postgraduate degree  
• 
Professional or academic programs  
However, the chatbot should describe these as examples rather than claiming that WiseIN 
provides a specific predefined category for each one unless the current form confirms it.

7. CURRENT EDUCATION 
A user may use their profile to communicate their current academic status. 
For example, a student may have an education entry representing their current college or 
university. 
The Education section should therefore be understood as profile information, not 
automatically as proof of student status. 
This distinction is important. 
Education profile information 
Means: 
Information a user has added to their WiseIN profile about their academic background. 
Student verification 
Means: 
A separate verification process associated with WiseIN's Student offering. 
These two concepts should not be treated as identical. 
 
8. EDUCATION VS STUDENT VERIFICATION 
The chatbot must clearly distinguish Education from Student Verification. 
A user can have education information on their profile without the chatbot automatically 
concluding that the user is student-verified. 
WiseIN's current public pricing information states that the Student plan requires a valid 
college ID + Aadhaar for the India-facing offering. It also provides a Student Badge on the 
profile.  
The current WiseIN homepage similarly describes: 
• 
Student Verification  
• 
Student Badge on Profile  

• 
Internship & Job Board Access  
• 
Campus Communities  
• 
Student Networking Events  
• 
Early Access to Student Opportunities  
for its Student offering.  
Therefore: 
Education ≠ Student Verification 
Education information ≠ verified academic credentials 
Adding a college/university to a profile should not automatically be described as 
verification unless the application explicitly shows a verified status. 
 
9. EDUCATION AND PROFILE VERIFICATION 
WiseIN is built around verified professional profiles. 
The public website describes WiseIN as a network where members are verified and 
explains government-based identity verification using methods such as Aadhaar, PAN, or 
DigiLocker.  
However, this does not mean that every Education entry is independently verified. 
The chatbot must distinguish: 
Identity verification 
from 
Education information 
from 
Student verification 
from 
Any education-specific verification status shown by the application. 

Unless the current product explicitly provides an education-verification indicator, the 
chatbot must not tell a user that their degree, college, CGPA, or academic history has been 
verified. 
 
10. EDITING EDUCATION 
WiseIN's Privacy Policy states that users can edit or delete certain information through 
their account settings.  
For Education entries, the chatbot should guide users through the current profile interface. 
General guidance: 
1. Open the WiseIN profile.  
2. Go to the Education section.  
3. Locate the education entry.  
4. Use the available edit/action control.  
5. Update the required information.  
6. Save the changes.  
The exact button name, icon, menu structure, and confirmation behavior should follow the 
current application. 
Do not invent an exact menu sequence if the authenticated application uses a 
different interface. 
 
11. DELETING EDUCATION 
If the current profile interface provides a delete/remove action for an education entry, the 
user can use that action to remove the entry. 
General guidance: 
1. Open the profile.  
2. Navigate to Education.  
3. Locate the relevant education entry.  
4. Open its available action menu.  

5. Select the available delete/remove option.  
6. Confirm if WiseIN asks for confirmation.  
If the user cannot find a delete option, the chatbot should not invent one. 
Instead: 
“The available actions can vary with the current WiseIN interface. Please check the actions 
available on your Education entry. If the option is missing or the entry cannot be removed, 
contact WiseIN support.”

15. EDUCATION AND PROFILE DISCOVERY 
Education information can contribute to the context of a user's professional profile. 

However, the chatbot should not claim that Education is definitely used as a search or 
ranking parameter unless that behavior is confirmed by the current WiseIN application or 
internal API documentation. 
WiseIN publicly states that users can discover individuals by skill, industry, and 
experience.  
Therefore, the safe chatbot statement is: 
“Your Education information is part of your profile and can provide academic context to 
people viewing your profile.” 
Do not state: 
“Adding a college will increase your search ranking.” 
unless WiseIN internally confirms that behavior. 
 
16. EDUCATION VISIBILITY AND PRIVACY 
Education is profile information. 
WiseIN's Privacy Policy states that some profile information may be visible to other WiseIN 
users depending on privacy settings. It specifically identifies information such as 
professional details and skills as public-profile information and states that users can 
control profile/data visibility through privacy settings.  
Therefore, when answering privacy-related Education questions, the chatbot should 
explain: 
“Education is profile information. Its visibility depends on WiseIN's current profile/privacy 
controls.” 
The chatbot should not promise that Education is: 
• 
Always public  
• 
Always private  
• 
Visible only to connections  
• 
Hidden from search  
• 
Visible only to verified users  
unless the current product settings explicitly confirm the behavior. 

 
17. EDUCATION DATA AND PRIVACY 
WiseIN states that it collects profile information users provide, including education, and 
allows users to edit or delete certain information through account settings.  
WiseIN also states that it uses profile and activity information for platform functions such 
as personalization of the feed, job recommendations, and connection suggestions.  
The chatbot should therefore treat Education information as user-provided profile data and 
avoid exposing private profile information to unauthorized users.

20. EDUCATION ENTRY NOT SAVING 
If a user reports: 
“My education is not saving.” 

Use the following troubleshooting flow. 
Step 1 — Check required fields 
Ask the user to make sure all fields currently marked as required in the form are completed. 
Do not invent required fields. 
Step 2 — Check the entered information 
Ask the user to check: 
• 
Spelling  
• 
Dates  
• 
Institution information  
• 
Any required selections  
• 
Any validation messages  
Step 3 — Retry 
Ask the user to: 
• 
Save again.  
• 
Refresh the page if appropriate.  
• 
Reopen the profile.  
• 
Check whether the entry appears.  
Step 4 — Check connectivity 
If the save operation fails: 
• 
Check the internet connection.  
• 
Retry after reconnecting.  
• 
Try refreshing the application.  
Step 5 — Browser/app troubleshooting 
If appropriate: 
• 
Refresh the page.  
• 
Sign out and sign in again.  

• 
Try a supported/current browser.  
• 
Try the WiseIN mobile app if the feature is available there.  
Step 6 — Escalation 
If the Education entry still cannot be saved, direct the user to WiseIN support. 
WiseIN's current Contact page provides a contact form and states that the team replies 
within 24 hours.  
 
21. EDUCATION INFORMATION DISAPPEARED 
If a user says: 
“My Education disappeared.” 
The chatbot should not immediately conclude that the data was deleted. 
Possible causes may include: 
• 
The entry was not successfully saved.  
• 
A profile update did not complete.  
• 
The user is viewing a different profile/account.  
• 
A temporary application issue occurred.  
• 
The entry was removed or edited.  
• 
A visibility setting affects what is displayed.  
The chatbot should first determine whether the Education entry is visible in the user's 
profile editing interface. 
If the information cannot be recovered through the normal profile controls, escalate to 
WiseIN support. 
 
22. INCORRECT EDUCATION INFORMATION 
If a user accidentally enters incorrect academic information: 
“Open your profile, go to the Education section, locate the relevant entry, and use the 
available edit control to update it.” 

If editing is unavailable: 
“If the current interface does not provide an edit option, contact WiseIN support for 
assistance.” 
Do not instruct the user to create duplicate entries unless the application requires that 
workflow.

separate features.  
 
User:

“Yes, the Education section is intended for adding academic information to your WiseIN 
profile. Open your profile and select ‘+ Add education’. The available fields are determined

User: 
“Can I add more than one education?” 
Safe response: 
“If the current Education section allows multiple entries, you can add your different 
academic experiences separately. The available number of entries is controlled by the 
current WiseIN application.”

30. COMMON USER QUESTIONS 
“Where can I add my education?” 
Go to your WiseIN profile and locate the Education section. Select “+ Add education.” 
“Can I edit my education?” 
If the current profile interface provides an edit action for the Education entry, use it to 
update the information. 
“Can I remove my education?” 
Use the available action on the Education entry if the current interface provides a 
remove/delete option. 
“Does adding education verify my degree?” 
No. Adding Education and verification are separate concepts. 
“Does adding my college give me the Student Badge?” 
Not automatically. Student Verification is a separate WiseIN feature. 
“Can I add my college even if I'm not using the Student plan?” 
Education is profile information. Student-plan eligibility and verification are separate from 
simply adding an Education entry. 
“Can other people see my education?” 

Education is profile information, and visibility depends on WiseIN's current profile/privacy 
controls. 
“Why can't I save my education?” 
Check the fields required by the current form, correct any validation messages, retry the 
save operation, and contact support if the problem continues.
