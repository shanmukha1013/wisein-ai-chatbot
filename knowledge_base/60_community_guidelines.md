# Community Guidelines

## Source: 60.pdf

0. Community Guidelines 
60.1 Overview 
WiseIN is designed as a verified professional networking platform where users can connect, 
communicate, share knowledge, discover opportunities, participate in communities, and conduct 
professional meetings. 
The Community Guidelines establish the expected standards of behavior for users across 
WiseIN, including posts, comments, Waves/clips, jobs, events, messages, communities, and 
meetings. 
The central principle is that WiseIN is built around real people, authentic identities, 
meaningful conversations, and professional interactions. WiseIN's published Content 
Policies emphasize that users should share genuine, useful content and avoid fake, harmful, 
misleading, spammy, or stolen content. 
 
60.2 Purpose of the Community Guidelines 
The guidelines are intended to: 
●​ Maintain a safe and professional community. 
●​ Encourage authentic interactions. 
●​ Prevent identity fraud and impersonation. 
●​ Reduce spam and unwanted communication. 
●​ Prevent scams and fraudulent opportunities. 
●​ Protect users' privacy. 
●​ Protect intellectual property and original content. 
●​ Maintain professional standards in communities. 
●​ Provide a reporting mechanism for policy violations. 
●​ Establish consequences for users who violate WiseIN policies. 
These principles complement WiseIN's broader platform rules concerning verified identities, 
professional communication, privacy, and prohibited activities. 
 
60.3 What Users Are Encouraged to Post 

WiseIN encourages users to share content that is genuine, useful, and relevant to professional 
or community interactions. 
Examples include: 
●​ Projects users are building. 
●​ Professional work and achievements. 
●​ Knowledge and skills. 
●​ Things users have learned. 
●​ Questions seeking help or advice. 
●​ Career opportunities. 
●​ Jobs and internships. 
●​ Events. 
●​ Professional experiences. 
●​ Personal achievements. 
●​ Honest challenges or struggles. 
●​ Information that could help other members. 
●​ Relevant community discussions. 
The basic principle is that content should be real, honest, and useful. 
 
60.4 Respectful and Safe Behavior 
Users are expected to communicate respectfully with other members. 
WiseIN prohibits behavior involving: 
●​ Harassment. 
●​ Bullying. 
●​ Threats. 
●​ Hate speech. 
●​ Discrimination. 
●​ Abusive behavior. 
●​ Defamatory behavior. 
●​ Violence or threats of violence. 
●​ Content intended to harm or exploit another person. 
Users should not target another person because of their identity or personal characteristics. 
WiseIN's Terms also prohibit unlawful, abusive, defamatory, harassing, threatening, 
discriminatory, and harmful activities. 

 
60.5 Prohibited Harmful Content 
The following categories of content are prohibited: 
Violence 
Users must not post or promote violent content or threats. 
Hate Speech 
Content attacking or promoting hatred against individuals or groups is prohibited. 
Discrimination 
Users must not promote discriminatory behavior or target people based on who they are. 
Sexually Explicit Content 
Sexually explicit material is not permitted on WiseIN. 
Disturbing or Graphic Content 
Users should not upload graphic, shocking, or disturbing material for attention or to provoke 
others. 
Harmful Content 
Content designed to harm, intimidate, exploit, or deliberately disturb other users is prohibited. 
 
60.6 Misinformation and False Information 
Users should not publish information that they know to be false or misleading. 
Examples include: 
●​ False claims. 
●​ Unverified claims presented as facts. 
●​ Fabricated statistics. 

●​ Rumors presented as factual information. 
●​ Manipulated screenshots. 
●​ Edited conversations presented misleadingly. 
●​ Fabricated professional information. 
Users are expected to verify information before presenting it as fact. 
If a user is uncertain about whether something is true, they should avoid presenting it as 
confirmed information. 
 
60.7 Authentic Identity 
Because WiseIN is based on verified professional identities, users are expected to represent 
themselves accurately. 
Users should: 
●​ Use their genuine identity. 
●​ Provide accurate professional information. 
●​ Represent their actual skills and experience. 
●​ Avoid exaggerating qualifications. 
●​ Avoid claiming expertise they do not possess. 
WiseIN's Terms require accurate account information and prohibit fake profiles and 
misrepresentation. 
 
60.8 Impersonation 
Users must not pretend to be another individual, company, organization, or entity. 
Prohibited behavior includes: 
●​ Using another person's identity. 
●​ Using another person's name or photograph. 
●​ Pretending to represent a company without authorization. 
●​ Creating misleading profiles. 
●​ Using another organization's identity deceptively. 
●​ Using unauthorized trademarked identities. 

Impersonation is treated as a serious violation because it undermines WiseIN's verified-network 
model. 
 
60.9 Fake Credentials and Professional 
Claims 
Users must not fabricate professional credentials. 
Examples include: 
●​ Fake certificates. 
●​ Fabricated employment history. 
●​ False educational qualifications. 
●​ False professional experience. 
●​ Fake achievements. 
●​ Manipulated documents. 
●​ Claiming qualifications that were never earned. 
Users are responsible for ensuring that their professional claims are truthful. 
 
60.10 Spam 
WiseIN prohibits spam and repetitive unwanted communication. 
Examples include: 
●​ Repeatedly posting the same content. 
●​ Copy-pasting the same post into multiple communities. 
●​ Sending repetitive messages. 
●​ Posting the same promotional material multiple times. 
●​ Repeatedly contacting users with unwanted offers. 
Users should create meaningful content rather than repeatedly publishing the same message. 
 

60.11 Self-Promotion 
Self-promotion is permitted when it provides relevant value. 
For example: 
"I built this application and here's what I learned." 
can be considered useful professional content. 
However, aggressive promotional behavior such as repeatedly pushing products, services, 
courses, or sales messages without meaningful context may be treated as spam. 
The important distinction is relevance and value. 
 
60.12 Links and External Promotion 
Users should not post irrelevant external links in: 
●​ Comments. 
●​ Communities. 
●​ Direct messages. 
●​ Other platform discussions. 
When sharing a link, users should explain why the link is relevant. 
WiseIN's published policy emphasizes context first, link second. 
 
60.13 Jobs and Recruitment 
Because WiseIN provides a verified recruitment environment, job and opportunity posts must be 
genuine. 
Job listings should represent: 
●​ A real position. 
●​ A real organization. 
●​ Genuine responsibilities. 

●​ Accurate compensation information. 
●​ Accurate employment conditions. 
Employers should not create fake job postings to collect personal information, generate leads, 
or deceive applicants. 
WiseIN's platform documentation describes verified corporate job postings and authenticated 
candidate profiles as part of its recruitment model. 
 
60.14 Honest Job Information 
Employers should clearly disclose important job conditions. 
For example: 
●​ If a position is unpaid → clearly state that it is unpaid. 
●​ If compensation is equity-only → clearly state it. 
●​ If the role is part-time → clearly state it. 
●​ If there are specific requirements → clearly describe them. 
Users should not disguise unfavorable employment conditions using misleading job titles or 
descriptions. 
 
60.15 Job Scams and Fraud 
The following are prohibited: 
●​ Fake job offers. 
●​ Fake internship offers. 
●​ Pyramid schemes. 
●​ Multi-level marketing schemes presented as employment. 
●​ Requests for payment to obtain a job. 
●​ Fraudulent work-from-home schemes. 
●​ Fake compensation promises. 
●​ Opportunities designed primarily to collect personal information. 
WiseIN specifically identifies scam offers and fraudulent employment schemes as prohibited 
content. 

 
60.16 Privacy of Other Users 
Users must respect the privacy of other members. 
Users should not publicly share another person's: 
●​ Phone number. 
●​ Email address. 
●​ Home address. 
●​ Personal information. 
●​ Private professional information. 
●​ Confidential information. 
This applies to posts, comments, messages, screenshots, and other forms of content. 
 
60.17 Private Conversations 
Private conversations should remain private unless all relevant participants provide permission 
to share them. 
Users should not publicly post: 
●​ Private chat screenshots. 
●​ Meeting conversations. 
●​ Private discussions. 
●​ Direct messages. 
●​ Confidential communications. 
WiseIN's Terms specifically prohibit unauthorized sharing of private conversations and 
meetings. 
 
60.18 Sensitive Information 
Users should avoid publicly sharing sensitive information such as: 

●​ Financial information. 
●​ Government ID documents. 
●​ Salary information. 
●​ Confidential company information. 
●​ Internal business strategies. 
●​ Private personal information. 
Sensitive information should only be shared with appropriate people and through appropriate 
channels. 
 
60.19 Video Calls and Meetings 
WiseIN provides video meetings and professional conversations. 
Users are expected to respect the privacy of participants. 
Meeting content should not be: 
●​ Secretly recorded. 
●​ Publicly uploaded without permission. 
●​ Shared with unrelated people. 
●​ Used to embarrass or harm participants. 
WiseIN's published Content Policies specifically state that meeting recordings, transcripts, and 
AI-generated summaries should not be shared without the relevant person's permission. 
 
60.20 Recording Consent 
Users should inform participants when a meeting is being recorded. 
Secretly recording another person is prohibited under WiseIN's published content rules. 
Before recording a meeting, users should obtain the required consent from participants. 
 
60.21 AI Meeting Summaries 

WiseIN can provide AI-generated meeting summaries and related meeting intelligence. 
These summaries should be treated as private meeting information. 
Users should not publicly post or distribute AI-generated meeting summaries without the 
consent of the relevant participants. 
 
60.22 Copyright and Ownership 
Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights to publish content. 
Users should primarily share: 
●​ Their own writing. 
●​ Their own images. 
●​ Their own videos. 
●​ Their own projects. 
●​ Content they have permission to use. 
WiseIN's policy states that users should not upload another person's work and claim it as their 
own. 
 
60.23 Plagiarism and Content Theft 
Users must not: 
●​ Copy articles and claim ownership. 
●​ Steal designs. 
●​ Upload pirated documents. 
●​ Copy another person's posts. 
●​ Reproduce another creator's work without authorization. 
●​ Present another person's work as their own. 
Where appropriate, users should give credit to the original creator or obtain permission before 
publishing the material. 
 

60.24 Logos and Brand Assets 
Users should not use another organization's: 
●​ Logo. 
●​ Brand assets. 
●​ Trademark. 
●​ Corporate imagery. 
without appropriate permission, particularly where such use could imply endorsement, 
ownership, or affiliation. 
Mentioning a company is different from using its protected brand assets. 
 
60.25 Community-Specific Rules 
Individual WiseIN communities may establish additional rules. 
For example: 
●​ A technology community may focus on programming. 
●​ An agriculture community may focus on agriculture. 
●​ A startup community may focus on entrepreneurship. 
Users joining a community should read and follow its specific rules. 
Community rules can exist in addition to WiseIN's platform-wide policies. 
 
60.26 Staying On Topic 
Users should keep community discussions relevant to the community's purpose. 
Users should avoid: 
●​ Hijacking unrelated discussions. 
●​ Posting unrelated promotional material. 
●​ Repeatedly changing the subject. 
●​ Using a community primarily for unrelated advertising. 

The objective is to preserve useful and focused discussions. 
 
60.27 Community Moderators 
Community creators or moderators may establish additional community rules and remove 
content that does not fit the community. 
Users are expected to respect legitimate moderation decisions. 
If a user disagrees with a moderation decision, they should address the issue appropriately 
rather than creating disruptive public arguments. 
 
60.28 Reporting Violations 
Users can report content or behavior that they believe violates WiseIN policies. 
Reportable content may include: 
●​ Posts. 
●​ Comments. 
●​ Job listings. 
●​ Events. 
●​ Messages. 
●​ Other inappropriate user behavior. 
WiseIN's Content Policies encourage users to report violations. 
 
60.29 Reporting Confidentiality 
According to WiseIN's published Content Policies, reports are treated confidentially. 
The policy states that the reported person will not be told who submitted the report and that 
reports are reviewed within 24 hours. 

This complements the 59. Reporting section you documented earlier, where users can select a 
reason such as: 
●​ Harassment 
●​ Spam 
●​ Inappropriate Content 
●​ Scam/Fraud 
●​ Impersonation 
●​ Violence 
●​ Other 
and provide details and optional evidence. 
 
60.30 Misuse of Reporting 
The reporting system should not be used to attack people simply because of disagreements. 
Users should not: 
●​ Mass-report someone as revenge. 
●​ Submit knowingly false reports. 
●​ Report legitimate content solely because they disagree with its viewpoint. 
●​ Abuse the reporting mechanism. 
False or malicious reporting can itself violate WiseIN's rules. 
 
60.31 Enforcement for Minor Violations 
WiseIN's published Content Policies describe a progressive approach for minor violations: 
First violation 
●​ Content may be removed. 
●​ User may receive a warning. 
Second violation 
●​ Temporary feature restrictions may be applied. 

Third violation 
●​ Account may undergo review. 
These measures are described for minor violations; serious violations may be handled 
differently. 
 
60.32 Serious Violations 
Certain violations may result in immediate action rather than the progressive process used for 
minor violations. 
Examples include: 
●​ Hate speech. 
●​ Harassment. 
●​ Scam posts. 
●​ Fake documents. 
●​ Privacy violations. 
Possible consequences include: 
●​ Account suspension. 
●​ Permanent account ban. 
WiseIN's policy states that serious violations can result in action even on a first offense. 
 
60.33 Severe Violations 
WiseIN identifies certain activities as severe violations that can result in permanent banning. 
These include: 
●​ Fraud or scam content. 
●​ Sexual content or advances. 
●​ Identity fraud. 
●​ Impersonation. 
●​ Data theft. 
●​ Scraping. 

●​ Threats. 
●​ Extortion. 
●​ Blackmail. 
●​ Publishing someone's private information. 
The published policy states that these violations can result in permanent bans and that WiseIN 
may report matters to legal authorities where required. 
 
60.34 Relationship With WiseIN 
Verification 
Community Guidelines are particularly important because WiseIN's platform is built around 
verified identities. 
The platform documentation describes WiseIN's core model as combining: 
●​ Identity verification. 
●​ Verified professional profiles. 
●​ Verified organizations. 
●​ Video-first connections. 
●​ Verified recruitment. 
●​ Spam-free messaging. 
Therefore, users are expected to maintain the same level of authenticity in their content and 
interactions as represented by their verified profiles. 
 
60.35 User Responsibility 
Every WiseIN member is responsible for the content and interactions associated with their 
account. 
Users should: 
1.​ Provide accurate information. 
2.​ Respect other members. 
3.​ Protect other people's privacy. 
4.​ Share legitimate content. 

5.​ Avoid spam. 
6.​ Avoid scams and fraudulent opportunities. 
7.​ Respect copyright. 
8.​ Follow community-specific rules. 
9.​ Report genuine violations. 
10.​Cooperate with platform moderation. 
WiseIN's Terms state that users are responsible for maintaining accurate account information 
and complying with platform requirements.

60.37 AI Customer Support Knowledge 
The WiseIN AI support chatbot should use this section when users ask about acceptable 
behavior, prohibited content, reporting, or community rules. 
Example: "What am I allowed to post?" 
Response: 
You can share genuine and useful content such as your work, projects, knowledge, 
questions, opportunities, events, achievements, and professional experiences. 
Avoid harmful, misleading, spammy, fraudulent, or stolen content. 
Example: "Can I promote my product?" 
Yes. Relevant self-promotion is allowed when it provides value. Avoid repetitive 
promotional posts, aggressive sales messages, or irrelevant links. 
Example: "Can I post someone's private conversation?" 
No. Private conversations, meeting discussions, recordings, transcripts, and AI 
summaries should not be shared publicly without the relevant participants' 
permission. 
Example: "Someone is harassing me. What should I do?" 

You can report the user through the Report User option on their profile. Select the 
appropriate reason, provide details, and optionally upload supporting evidence. 
Example: "What happens after I report someone?" 
WiseIN's published Content Policies state that reports are reviewed within 24 hours 
and that the identity of the person who submitted the report is kept confidential.
