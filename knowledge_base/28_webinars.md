1. WEBINARS — OVERVIEW 
A Webinar on WiseIN is a professional online session designed for sharing knowledge, 
learning, presentations, discussions, or other structured professional content with an 
audience. 
The current WiseIN profile interface includes a dedicated Webinars tab. In the supplied 
current profile screenshot, the tab displays the message: 
“Upcoming webinar sessions, learning events, and registrations will appear here once 
published.” 
This establishes that the Webinars section can be associated with upcoming webinar 
sessions, learning events, and registrations. 
WiseIN's public website also explicitly lists Webinar Hosting as an Organization feature. 
The current India Organization plan includes Webinar Hosting and states a 2.05% fee for 
webinars with up to 100 participants, with WiseIN asking organizations to discuss 
arrangements for larger audiences.  
Therefore, the chatbot should treat Webinars as a distinct WiseIN feature related to 
professional learning, presentations, hosted sessions, and registrations, while keeping it 
separate from ordinary 1-to-1 meetings, Sessions, Audio Rooms, and public Events.

41. WEBINAR TROUBLESHOOTING 
Problem: “I cannot see the Webinars tab.” 
Possible explanations: 
• 
the profile UI may have changed  
• 
the feature may not be available in the current account/product state  
• 
the user may be viewing a different profile type  
• 
the application may not have loaded correctly.  
Recommended troubleshooting: 
1. Refresh the profile.  
2. Reopen the profile.  
3. Check whether the Webinars tab appears.  
4. Try the latest WiseIN application/web version.  

5. If the issue continues, contact WiseIN support.  
Do not claim that the tab is guaranteed for every profile. 
 
Problem: “The Webinars tab is empty.” 
Explain: 
The current profile may simply have no published upcoming webinar sessions or 
registration information available. 
An empty section does not prove that the user has never hosted a webinar. 
 
Problem: “I cannot register for a webinar.” 
Check: 
• 
whether the webinar is still open for registration  
• 
whether the webinar is upcoming  
• 
whether the webinar is cancelled  
• 
whether the registration option is currently available  
• 
whether the user's account/session is authenticated.  
If live webinar APIs are available, retrieve the current registration state. 
 
Problem: “The webinar page is not loading.” 
Recommended troubleshooting: 
1. Refresh the page.  
2. Check internet connectivity.  
3. Log in again if necessary.  
4. Try another supported browser/device.  
5. Check whether WiseIN itself is experiencing a service issue.  
6. Contact WiseIN support if the problem continues.  

 
Problem: “I registered but cannot find the webinar.” 
The chatbot should check the user's authorized registration data. 
Possible states include: 
• 
registration exists  
• 
registration failed  
• 
webinar cancelled  
• 
webinar completed  
• 
registration not found.  
Do not claim a registration exists without querying the authorized backend.

49. GOLDEN CHATBOT ANSWERS 
Q: What is a webinar on WiseIN? 
Answer: 
A WiseIN webinar is a professional online session intended for structured learning, 
presentations, discussions, or knowledge sharing. The Webinars section on a profile can 
display upcoming webinar sessions, learning events, and registration information when 
published. 
 
Q: Where can I find someone's webinars? 
Answer: 
Open the person's WiseIN profile and check the Webinars tab. Published upcoming 
webinar sessions and related registration information can appear there. 

 
Q: My Webinars tab is empty. Why? 
Answer: 
The profile may not currently have any published upcoming webinar sessions or 
registration information. An empty Webinars section does not necessarily mean the person 
has never hosted a webinar. 
 
Q: Can organizations host webinars on WiseIN? 
Answer: 
Yes. WiseIN's current Organization offering explicitly includes Webinar Hosting.  
 
Q: How much does webinar hosting cost? 
Answer: 
WiseIN's current India Organization offering is ₹1,499/month and includes Webinar 
Hosting. The website also lists a 2.05% fee for webinars with up to 100 participants and 
asks users to discuss larger webinars with WiseIN.  
 
Q: Are webinars the same as meetings? 
Answer: 
Not necessarily. WiseIN treats Webinars as a separate feature from ordinary video 
meetings and profile Sessions. Webinars are oriented toward structured sessions, learning, 
presentations, and registrations. 
 
Q: Does every WiseIN user get webinar hosting? 
Answer: 
WiseIN's current public pricing specifically lists Webinar Hosting under the Organization 
offering. The public information does not establish that every individual plan includes 
webinar hosting.  

 
Q: Are webinars automatically recorded? 
Answer: 
WiseIN publicly describes AI meeting summaries and meeting intelligence, but the 
available public documentation does not establish that every webinar is automatically 
recorded. The specific webinar's current capabilities should be checked in the platform. 
 
Q: Are webinars automatically transcribed? 
Answer: 
WiseIN advertises AI meeting summaries and transcription for supported Premium 
experiences, but this should not be assumed to apply automatically to every webinar. 
Check the specific webinar's available features.
