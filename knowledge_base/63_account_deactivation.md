# Account Deactivation

## Source: 63.pdf

63. Account Deactivation 
WiseIN Account Deactivation 
The Deactivate Account feature allows a WiseIN user to temporarily pause their account 
without permanently deleting their account or losing their data. 
63.1 Purpose 
Account deactivation is intended for users who want to temporarily stop using WiseIN while 
keeping their account and data available for future reactivation. 
The page explicitly states: 
“Temporarily pause your WiseIN account without losing your data. You can 
reactivate anytime and pick up right where you left off.” 
 
63.2 How to Deactivate an Account 
Users can access the feature through: 
Settings → Deactivate 
The Settings menu contains the Deactivate option alongside other account-management 
options such as: 
●​ Edit Profile 
●​ Notifications 
●​ Sign in & Security 
●​ Subscriptions 
●​ Data Privacy 
●​ Scheduled Post 
●​ Saved Content 
●​ My Ads 
●​ Blocked 
●​ Deactivate 
●​ Delete Account 
●​ Log Out 

When the user selects Deactivate, the Account Deactivation page is displayed. 
 
63.3 Deactivation Page 
The page contains: 
Heading 
Deactivate Account 
Description 
The page explains that users can temporarily pause their WiseIN account without losing their 
data. 
It also states that the account can be reactivated at any time, allowing the user to continue 
from where they left off. 
Action 
Deactivate Account 
A red Deactivate Account button is provided to initiate the deactivation process. 
 
63.4 Deactivation vs. Account Deletion 
These are separate account-management options in WiseIN. 
Deactivate Account 
●​ Temporarily pauses the account. 
●​ Does not involve permanent account deletion. 
●​ User data is retained. 
●​ Account can be reactivated. 
●​ User can continue from where they left off. 
Delete Account 
●​ A separate option available in Settings. 
●​ Intended for permanently deleting the account rather than temporarily pausing it. 

Important: The provided screen confirms the temporary nature of deactivation and retention of 
data, but it does not specify the exact visibility restrictions or behavior of individual features 
while an account is deactivated. 
 
63.5 Reactivating the Account 
According to the displayed information, a deactivated account can be reactivated at any time. 
After reactivation, the user can: 
●​ Return to their WiseIN account. 
●​ Continue from where they previously stopped. 
●​ Retain their existing data. 
The screenshots do not specify the exact steps or button used for reactivation, so those details 
should not be assumed in the chatbot knowledge base. 
 
63.6 Chatbot Knowledge 
User: What is account deactivation? 
WiseIN: Account deactivation temporarily pauses your WiseIN account without losing your data. 
You can reactivate your account at any time and continue from where you left off. 
User: Will I lose my data if I deactivate my account? 
WiseIN: No. The Deactivate Account page states that you can temporarily pause your WiseIN 
account without losing your data. 
User: Can I reactivate my account? 
WiseIN: Yes. A deactivated WiseIN account can be reactivated at any time. 
User: Where can I deactivate my account? 
WiseIN: Go to Settings → Deactivate, then select Deactivate Account. 
User: Is deactivation permanent? 

WiseIN: No. Deactivation is described as a temporary pause. You can reactivate your account 
later. 
User: Is deactivation the same as deleting my account? 
WiseIN: No. WiseIN provides separate Deactivate and Delete Account options. Deactivation 
temporarily pauses the account, while deletion is a separate account-management action. 
User: I want to temporarily stop using WiseIN. What should I do? 
WiseIN: You can use Settings → Deactivate to temporarily pause your account without losing 
your data. You can reactivate it later. 
 
63.7 Important Support Limitation 
The available screen does not provide details about: 
●​ Whether the profile becomes invisible after deactivation. 
●​ What happens to existing messages. 
●​ What happens to posts, comments, or connections. 
●​ Whether subscriptions continue during deactivation. 
●​ Whether scheduled posts are paused. 
●​ Whether notifications stop. 
●​ The exact reactivation procedure. 
●​ Whether there is a confirmation popup before deactivation. 
These should be documented separately only after they are verified in the WiseIN application.
