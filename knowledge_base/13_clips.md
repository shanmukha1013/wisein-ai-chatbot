1. OVERVIEW — WHAT ARE CLIPS ON WISEIN? 
1.1 What is a Clip? 
A Clip is a video-oriented content format available on WiseIN. 
WiseIN's official app documentation explicitly states that users can "Post updates, clips, 
and articles."  
Clips are therefore part of WiseIN's content-sharing experience and can be used to 
communicate professional, educational, technical, creative, or community-oriented 
information through video. 
 
2. PURPOSE OF CLIPS 
Clips can be used to communicate information that is better demonstrated visually or 
through video. 
Appropriate examples include: 
• 
Project demonstrations  
• 
Product demonstrations  
• 
Technical demonstrations  
• 
Tutorials  
• 
Educational explanations  
• 
Professional experiences  
• 
Career-related content  
• 
Creative work  
• 
Technology updates  
• 
Event-related content  
• 
Demonstrations of something the user has built  

• 
Useful knowledge  
• 
Professional achievements  
A Clip should provide genuine value rather than being created solely to generate spam or 
misleading engagement. 
WiseIN's Content Policies apply to Clips as part of the platform's content ecosystem.  
 
3. CLIPS AND THE WISEIN HOME FEED 
WiseIN's official mobile-app documentation describes the Home Feed as a place for 
updates and events and separately confirms that users can post Clips.  
The current WiseIN Home page also provides a New Post entry point and displays 
content/posts.  
The exact location where a Clip appears can depend on the current WiseIN interface and 
feed configuration. 
AI rule 
Do not tell a user that a Clip will definitely appear in: 
• 
Trending  
• 
Following  
• 
A dedicated Clips feed  
• 
A particular ranking position  
unless the current product interface or an authorized WiseIN API confirms it. 
 
4. CLIP VS REGULAR POST 
A regular post/update is primarily a general content-sharing format. 
A Clip is a video-oriented format. 
Both can be used for professional and useful content. 
Example 
A user building an AI project could: 

Create a regular post: 
"I built an AI-powered project auditor that detects security and code-quality issues." 
Create a Clip: 
Record a short video demonstrating the project detecting an issue and showing the result. 
The chatbot should explain the distinction in terms of content format, not claim that Clips 
necessarily receive higher or lower ranking.

Impersonate another person  
•

Claim qualifications you do not possess  
• 
Display fake certificates  
• 
Claim work that somebody else completed as your own  
• 
Create misleading professional credentials  
WiseIN's Content Policies specifically prohibit impersonation and fake credentials.

13. MEETING RECORDINGS AND CLIPS

WiseIN's Content Policies state that recordings, transcripts, and AI-generated meeting

involved.  
Chatbot response 
"If your Clip contains a recording of another person's meeting or conversation, make sure 
everyone involved has given appropriate permission before publishing it." 
 

14. COPYRIGHT AND CLIPS 
Users should only upload video content that: 
• 
They created themselves, or  
• 
They have permission to use.  
Do not upload: 
• 
Pirated videos  
• 
Someone else's video without permission  
• 
Copyrighted music/content without appropriate rights  
• 
Reproduced content presented as your own  
WiseIN's Content Policies require users to respect copyright and ownership.  
 
15. MUSIC AND THIRD-PARTY CONTENT 
If a user asks: 
"Can I use this song in my Clip?" 
The chatbot should not automatically say yes. 
Recommended response: 
"Only use music or other third-party content when you have the appropriate rights or 
permission to use it. WiseIN's Content Policies require users to respect copyright and 
ownership." 
 
16. CLIPS AND JOB/INTERNSHIP PROMOTIONS 
Users may use content to communicate genuine professional opportunities, but job and 
internship content must comply with WiseIN's opportunity rules. 
A Clip promoting an opportunity should not be used to promote: 
• 
Fake jobs  
• 
Fake internships  
• 
Lead-generation scams  

• 
MLM schemes  
• 
Pyramid schemes  
• 
Pay-to-get-a-job schemes  
• 
Misleading compensation claims  
WiseIN states that jobs, gigs, internships, and opportunities must be genuine and that 
important conditions such as unpaid, equity-only, or part-time status should be clearly 
disclosed.  
 
17. CLIPS AND SPAM 
Do not repeatedly upload substantially identical Clips merely to increase visibility. 
Examples of spam: 
• 
Re-uploading the same Clip repeatedly  
• 
Posting identical promotional videos in multiple places without relevance  
• 
Repeatedly promoting the same offer  
• 
Using misleading content to attract clicks  
WiseIN's Content Policies prohibit spam and repetitive copy-paste content.

23. CLIP VISIBILITY 
The chatbot must distinguish between: 
Published 
The Clip has been submitted successfully. 
Visible 
The Clip is available to users in the relevant WiseIN experience. 
Distributed 
The platform may show the Clip to users through its content/feed systems. 
Trending 
The Clip may be surfaced as trending content if the current platform supports such 
distribution. 
These are not interchangeable terms. 

The chatbot must not tell a user: 
"Your Clip is definitely visible." 
unless an authorized WiseIN API confirms its status.

29. WHY IS MY CLIP NOT UPLOADING? 
Possible causes include: 
• 
Unsupported video format  
• 
File too large  
• 
Corrupted video  
• 
Temporary network problem  
• 
Temporary platform issue  
• 
Session/authentication problem  
• 
Account or feature restriction  
• 
Current uploader error  
Troubleshooting 
1. Check the file requirements displayed by the current uploader.  
2. Try a supported video file.  
3. Reduce the file size if necessary.  
4. Check the internet connection.  
5. Refresh WiseIN.  
6. Sign in again if required.  

7. Try uploading again.  
8. Contact WiseIN Support if the problem continues.  
Do not state a specific technical cause unless the application provides an error message 
identifying it. 
 
30. WHY IS MY CLIP NOT VISIBLE? 
If a user says: 
"I uploaded my Clip but I can't see it." 
Ask them to: 
1. Refresh the Home page.  
2. Check their own profile/content area if available.  
3. Check whether a feed filter is active.  
4. Confirm that the upload/publishing process completed.  
5. Check for an error or moderation notification.  
6. Retry after checking their internet connection.  
The current Home page can itself show "Failed to load posts", so a temporary loading 
problem is one possible explanation.  
Do not automatically tell the user that the Clip was removed or hidden.

32. REPORTING A CLIP 
If a Clip violates WiseIN's policies, the user should use the available Report functionality in 
the current interface. 
General process: 
1. Open the Clip's options menu.  
2. Select Report, if available.  
3. Select the relevant reason.  
4. Submit the report.  
WiseIN's Content Policies state that reports are confidential and that reports are reviewed 
within 24 hours. The policy explicitly covers reporting content such as posts, comments, 
job listings, events, and messages; the chatbot should not invent an additional fixed Clip-
specific reporting workflow if the UI differs.  
 
33. REPORTING MUST NOT BE ABUSED

Disagree with their opinion  
• 
Dislike their content  

• 
Have a personal disagreement  
WiseIN states that misuse of reporting, including false or mass reporting, can itself violate 
its rules.

45. COMMON CLIP QUESTIONS 
Q: What is a Clip? 
Answer: 
A Clip is a video-oriented content format on WiseIN. WiseIN supports posting Clips 
alongside updates and articles. 
 
Q: Can I use Clips to showcase my project? 
Answer: 
Yes. A project demonstration is a suitable use case for a Clip, provided the content is 
genuine, relevant, and compliant with WiseIN's Content Policies. 

 
Q: Can I promote my work using a Clip? 
Answer: 
Yes. WiseIN allows relevant, value-adding self-promotion. Avoid repetitive spam or 
aggressive promotional content. 
 
Q: What is the maximum Clip duration? 
Answer: 
The current public WiseIN documentation I have does not specify a verified maximum Clip 
duration. Please use the duration limit shown by the current Clip uploader. 
 
Q: What is the maximum Clip file size? 
Answer: 
The current public WiseIN documentation I have does not specify a verified Clip file-size 
limit. Please check the requirements displayed by the current uploader. 
 
Q: What video format should I upload? 
Answer: 
Use a video format accepted by the current WiseIN Clip uploader. If your upload is 
rejected, check the format and file requirements displayed by the uploader. 
 
Q: My Clip is not uploading. What should I do? 
Answer: 
Check the current uploader's format and size requirements, verify your internet 
connection, try another compatible video, refresh WiseIN, and retry. If the problem 
continues, contact WiseIN Support. 
 
Q: My Clip disappeared. Was it deleted? 

Answer: 
Not necessarily. It could be a temporary loading issue, a visibility/filter issue, or a 
moderation action. I can't determine the actual status without access to your WiseIN 
account or an authorized status tool. 
 
Q: Why was my Clip removed? 
Answer: 
WiseIN may remove content that violates its Content Policies. Possible categories include 
spam, misinformation, harmful content, privacy violations, impersonation, scams, or 
copyright violations. The exact reason for your Clip requires the relevant WiseIN 
moderation information.
