## Source: 64.pdf

64. Account Deletion 
Overview 
The Delete Account section allows WiseIN users to permanently remove their WiseIN account 
and associated data. 
This option is different from Account Deactivation. Deactivation temporarily pauses the 
account and allows the user to return later, while deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. 
Location 
Users can access this option through: 
Settings → Delete Account 
Page Title 
Delete Account 
Description 
The page informs the user: 
“Permanently remove your account and all your data from WiseIN. This action 
cannot be undone, so make sure you really want to delete it.” 
Available Action 
Delete Account 
A red Delete Account button is displayed. 
Selecting this option is intended to permanently remove the user's WiseIN account and data. 
Important Information 
●​ Account deletion is permanent. 
●​ The action cannot be undone according to the displayed page. 
●​ The user should make sure they genuinely want to delete the account before 
proceeding. 
●​ The page states that all user data will be removed from WiseIN.

AI Customer Support Knowledge 
User: How can I delete my WiseIN account?​
 Bot: Go to Settings → Delete Account and select Delete Account. Account deletion is 
permanent and cannot be undone. 
User: Can I recover my account after deleting it?​
 Bot: The Delete Account page states that account deletion cannot be undone. 

User: Does deleting my account delete my data?​
 Bot: The page states that deleting your account will permanently remove your account and 
all your data from WiseIN. 
User: What is the difference between deactivating and deleting my account?​
 Bot: Deactivation temporarily pauses your WiseIN account while keeping your data so you 
can reactivate it later. Deletion permanently removes your account and data and cannot be 
undone. 
User: I don't want to permanently delete my account. What should I do?​
 Bot: If you only want to take a temporary break from WiseIN, use Settings → Deactivate 
instead of Delete Account.
