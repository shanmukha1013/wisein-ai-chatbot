1. FEATURE OVERVIEW 
1.1 What is AI Meeting Summaries? 
AI Meeting Summaries is a WiseIN meeting-intelligence feature that automatically 
generates an AI-powered summary after a video meeting/call. 
WiseIN's current official website describes the feature as: 
• 
After every call, AI writes a full summary.  
• 
The summary includes key points.  
• 
It includes follow-ups.  
• 
It includes action items.  
• 
The purpose is to allow participants to focus on the conversation instead of 
manually taking notes.   
The official WiseIN mobile-app page also states that users can read AI meeting 
summaries after every call.  
Simple chatbot definition 
AI Meeting Summaries automatically creates an AI-generated summary of a WiseIN 
video meeting after the call, highlighting key points, follow-ups, and action items. 
 
2. WHAT IS MEETING INTELLIGENCE? 

WiseIN describes this functionality as Meeting intelligence. 
The current WiseIN website presents the meeting experience as: 
Video Call → Conversation → AI-generated Meeting Summary → Key Points + Follow-ups 
+ Action Items 
The website specifically presents an example of a meeting summary showing 3 action 
items.  
WiseIN's About page similarly explains that after a call, AI generates: 
• 
Meeting summary  
• 
Key points  
• 
Follow-ups  
• 
Action items   
Therefore, when a user asks: 
"What is meeting intelligence?" 
the chatbot should explain: 
Meeting intelligence is WiseIN's AI-powered capability that turns information from a 
video meeting into a structured meeting summary, including key points, follow-ups, 
and action items. 
 
3. WHY AI MEETING SUMMARIES EXIST 
The primary purpose is to reduce the need for manual note-taking. 
WiseIN's public website presents the feature around the idea that users can: 
• 
focus on the conversation,  
• 
avoid manually taking notes,  
• 
review important information after the call,  
• 
remember follow-ups,  
• 
identify action items.  

The current homepage explicitly describes the experience as AI taking notes so the user 
can remain focused on the conversation.  
Chatbot explanation 
If a user asks: 
"Why does WiseIN generate meeting summaries?" 
Answer: 
WiseIN uses AI meeting intelligence to summarize important information from your video 
call so you can review the key points, follow-ups, and action items without relying entirely 
on manual note-taking. 
 
4. WHAT DOES THE AI SUMMARY CONTAIN? 
Based on the current official WiseIN website, the AI meeting summary includes three 
explicitly identified categories. 
4.1 Key Points 
Key points represent important information discussed during the meeting. 
Examples of possible content: 
• 
topics discussed,  
• 
important decisions,  
• 
important observations,  
• 
major discussion points.  
Important chatbot rule 
Do not claim that WiseIN always uses a specific number of key points or a fixed formatting 
structure unless the current product/API confirms it. 
 
5. FOLLOW-UPS 
The official WiseIN website identifies follow-ups as part of the AI-generated meeting 
summary.  
A follow-up represents something that needs attention after the meeting. 

For example: 
• 
contacting someone,  
• 
sending information,  
• 
continuing a discussion,  
• 
arranging another interaction.  
Chatbot response 
WiseIN's AI meeting summary can identify follow-ups from the conversation so you can 
keep track of what needs attention after the meeting. 
Do not claim that WiseIN automatically creates calendar events, reminders, tasks, emails, 
or notifications from follow-ups unless an approved WiseIN source confirms that behavior. 
 
6. ACTION ITEMS 
The official website explicitly identifies action items as part of meeting intelligence.  
An action item is a task or next step identified from the conversation. 
For example: 
• 
prepare a document,  
• 
share project information,  
• 
contact a person,  
• 
complete a technical task,  
• 
continue a discussion.  
The current WiseIN homepage even shows a meeting-summary example containing 3 
action items.  
Important distinction 
The chatbot should understand: 
Key Points = What was important in the discussion. 
Follow-ups = What needs attention after the discussion. 
Action Items = Specific tasks or next steps identified from the meeting. 

These are useful conceptual distinctions, but the chatbot must not claim that WiseIN 
exposes them as separate UI sections unless the live product confirms it.

15. PRIVACY OF AI MEETING SUMMARIES 
This is one of the most important chatbot rules. 
WiseIN's current Content Policies explicitly state: 
AI meeting summaries are personal. 
The policy says the AI meeting summary is for the user and should not be posted publicly 
or shared without the other person's consent.  
The same policy states that WiseIN video-call content should remain between the people 
on the call and that recordings, transcripts, or AI summaries should not be shared without 
explicit permission from the other person.  
Chatbot rule 
If the user asks: 
"Can I post my AI meeting summary on WiseIN?" 
Answer: 
WiseIN's Content Policies say AI meeting summaries are personal. You should not post or 
share a meeting summary publicly without the other participant's consent.
