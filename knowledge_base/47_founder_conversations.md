1. DOCUMENT PURPOSE 
This document defines the knowledge that the WiseIN AI chatbot can use when answering 
questions about Founder Conversations. 
Founder Conversations should be understood as part of WiseIN's broader video-first 
professional networking and conversation experience, where users can discover 
verified professionals and connect through scheduled video conversations rather than 
relying only on text-based networking. 
WiseIN's homepage explicitly positions the platform around connecting face-to-face with 
founders, developers, and creators who are verified, and describes its model as 
discovering the right person and scheduling a video call.  
This document combines: 
• 
Current official WiseIN website information  
• 
WiseIN public event information  
• 
WiseIN pricing and product information  
• 
WiseIN Terms and Content Policies  
• 
The supplied WiseIN profile screenshot showing the Sessions interface  
• 
Previously prepared WiseIN knowledge-base documents  
Where the public website does not establish an exact workflow or rule, the chatbot must 
not invent one. 
 
2. WHAT ARE FOUNDER CONVERSATIONS? 
Founder Conversations are conversations centered around founders, entrepreneurship, 
business building, ideas, challenges, professional experiences, and meaningful 
networking. 

WiseIN's public website specifically highlights face-to-face connections with founders, 
developers, and creators, and describes video-first conversations as a core part of the 
platform.  
WiseIN also has a public event titled: 
“WiseIn Founder Conversations” 
The public event page describes it as: 
• 
Online  
• 
Jan–June 2026  
• 
Verified, approval-based conversations  
• 
With a Subscribe option  
• 
With an Events / Calendar interface.  
Important chatbot interpretation 
The public WiseIn Founder Conversations event page is evidence of a Founder 
Conversations event/program format. 
However, the supplied profile screenshot also shows individual Sessions such as: 
• 
“Founder Connect: Let’s Talk”  
• 
“Discovery Call with Santhosh...”  
• 
“Career Discussion with Santhosh...”  
• 
“Let’s Connect & Have a Conversation”  
These are visible under the profile's Sessions tab. 
The chatbot should therefore distinguish between: 
Founder Conversations / Founder-focused events 
and 
individual scheduled Sessions / video meetings 
unless the current WiseIN API explicitly confirms that a particular session belongs to a 
specific Founder Conversations event. 
 
3. WISEIN'S VIDEO-FIRST CONVERSATION MODEL 

Founder Conversations are consistent with WiseIN's broader product design. 
WiseIN describes its platform as a verified professional network where users can:

3. Schedule a video conversation.

5. Receive AI-generated meeting intelligence after calls.

• 
Manage availability and slot bookings  
• 
Discover verified professionals by skill  
•

Therefore, Founder Conversations should be treated as a professional conversation

31. EXAMPLE USER QUESTIONS AND GOLDEN ANSWERS 

Q: What are Founder Conversations? 
Answer: 
Founder Conversations are founder-focused professional conversations on WiseIN. WiseIN 
is designed around verified, face-to-face professional networking, and its public Founder 
Conversations event has been described as involving verified, approval-based 
conversations.  
 
Q: Is Founder Conversations a job? 
Answer: 
No. Founder Conversations are conversation and networking opportunities, not job listings. 
A conversation may discuss careers, business, or opportunities, but actual jobs should be 
checked in WiseIN's Jobs or Organization Jobs areas. 
 
Q: Can I talk to a founder on WiseIN? 
Answer: 
WiseIN is designed to help users connect face-to-face with verified professionals, 
including founders. Specific founder sessions and availability should be checked from the 
current profile or session listings.  
 
Q: Are Founder Conversations online? 
Answer: 
The publicly listed WiseIn Founder Conversations event was shown as an online event. 
Individual sessions can also be presented as video meetings, as shown in the supplied 
WiseIN Sessions interface.  
 
Q: How long is a Founder Conversation? 
Answer: 

The supplied WiseIN Sessions interface shows the displayed conversation sessions as 30-
minute video meetings. The duration of a specific Founder Conversation should be 
checked from its current session listing. 
 
Q: Are Founder Conversations free? 
Answer: 
Some conversation sessions shown in the supplied WiseIN interface are marked 
“Complimentary.” However, that should not be treated as a universal rule for every Founder 
Conversation. Check the specific current session for its applicable pricing and eligibility. 
 
Q: Do I need approval? 
Answer: 
The publicly listed WiseIn Founder Conversations event is described as using verified, 
approval-based conversations. The exact approval criteria are not publicly documented in 
the source available here.  
 
Q: Can I record the conversation? 
Answer: 
WiseIN requires participants to know if a call is being recorded. Secret recording is not 
permitted, and recordings, transcripts, or AI summaries should not be shared without the 
other participant's explicit permission.  
 
Q: Does WiseIN create an AI summary? 
Answer: 
WiseIN describes AI meeting intelligence that can generate meeting summaries, key 
points, follow-ups, and action items after calls.  
 
Q: Can I share the AI summary publicly? 
Answer: 

No. WiseIN's Content Policies state that AI meeting summaries are personal and should 
not be publicly shared without the other participant's consent.  
 
Q: What is Founder Connect: Let's Talk? 
Answer: 
In the supplied WiseIN profile interface, “Founder Connect: Let’s Talk” appears under the 
Sessions section as a conversation offering described as an open conversation about 
building, ideas, and challenges. The displayed session is a 30-minute complimentary video 
meeting. Availability and current details should be checked from the live session listing.
