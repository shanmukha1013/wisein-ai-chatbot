1. FEATURE OVERVIEW 
Saved Content is a WiseIN feature available under Settings that allows users to access 
content they have previously saved for later reference. 
The Saved Content section organizes saved content into two separate categories: 
●​ Posts 
●​ Clips 
Users can switch between these categories using the tabs available at the top of the Saved 
Content page. 
 
2. PURPOSE OF THE FEATURE 
The Saved Content feature provides users with a centralized location to revisit content they 
found useful or want to access later. 
Instead of searching for previously viewed content again, users can open the Saved Content 
section and browse their saved Posts and Clips. 
 
3. NAVIGATION 
Primary Navigation Path 

WiseIN → Settings → Saved Content 
Navigation Steps 
1.​ Log in to the WiseIN account. 
2.​ Open Settings from the main navigation. 
3.​ Select Saved Content. 
4.​ The Saved Content page opens. 
5.​ Select Posts or Clips depending on the type of content the user wants to view.

5. SAVED POSTS 
The Posts section contains posts that the user has saved. 
A saved post may display information such as: 
●​ Creator profile picture 
●​ Creator name 
●​ Time/date information 
●​ Post title or text 
●​ Hashtags 

●​ Post media 
●​ Additional content options 
Example Observed 
The Posts section can display saved content such as: 
Creator: Santosh Vanaparthi​
 Post: Why I started WiseIn?​
 Hashtags: #wisein #entrepreneurship #networking #startuplife #startup 
The saved post may contain media associated with the original post. 
 
6. SAVED CLIPS 
The Clips section contains clips that the user has saved. 
A saved Clip may display: 
●​ Creator profile picture 
●​ Creator name 
●​ Time/date information 
●​ Clip content 
●​ Description or title 
●​ Hashtags 
●​ Video/media 
●​ Content options 
Clips containing video content can be viewed directly from the Saved Content page using the 
available playback controls. 
 
7. SWITCHING BETWEEN POSTS AND CLIPS 
Users can change the displayed content by selecting the appropriate tab. 
To view saved Posts 
Saved Content → Posts 
To view saved Clips 

Saved Content → Clips 
Only the content belonging to the selected category is displayed. 
 
8. SAVED CONTENT ITEM 
Each saved content item is displayed as an individual content card/post. 
Depending on the content type, the item may contain: 
Creator Information

●​ Image or video content 
Content Controls

An individual content item may display a three-dot menu (⋮) for additional actions.

10. USER USE CASES 
Saved Content can be used when a user wants to: 
●​ Revisit an interesting post. 
●​ Rewatch a saved clip. 
●​ Keep useful content for later reference. 
●​ Find previously saved content without searching for it again. 
●​ Separate saved Posts from saved Clips. 
 
11. COMMON USER QUESTIONS 
Q1. Where can I find my saved content? 
Go to: 
Settings → Saved Content 
From there, select Posts or Clips. 
 
Q2. Where can I find my saved posts? 
Go to: 
Settings → Saved Content → Posts 
 
Q3. Where can I find my saved clips? 
Go to: 

Settings → Saved Content → Clips 
 
Q4. I saved a post earlier. Where can I find it? 
Open: 
Settings → Saved Content → Posts 
Your saved posts should appear in this section. 
 
Q5. I saved a clip. Where can I find it? 
Open: 
Settings → Saved Content → Clips 
Your saved clips should appear there. 
 
Q6. Can I access my saved Posts and Clips from the same page? 
Yes. Both categories are available under Saved Content, with separate Posts and Clips tabs. 
 
12. TROUBLESHOOTING 
Issue 1: Saved Content is not loading 
Recommended steps: 
1.​ Refresh the WiseIN page. 
2.​ Check the internet connection. 
3.​ Open Settings again. 
4.​ Select Saved Content. 
5.​ Try switching between Posts and Clips. 
If the issue continues, collect the error details and escalate to Customer Support. 
 

Issue 2: Saved Post is not visible 
Verify that the user is checking: 
Settings → Saved Content → Posts 
If the post is still missing, additional investigation may be required. 
 
Issue 3: Saved Clip is not visible 
Verify that the user is checking: 
Settings → Saved Content → Clips 
If the clip is still missing, collect the relevant details and escalate if necessary. 
 
Issue 4: Saved video does not play 
Check: 
1.​ Internet connectivity. 
2.​ Whether other videos are playing correctly. 
3.​ Whether the issue occurs with one specific Clip or multiple Clips. 
If the issue persists, escalate it to Customer Support with the relevant details.

14. AI RESPONSE RULES 
When answering questions about Saved Content, the AI Assistant should: 
DO 
●​ Provide the exact navigation path. 
●​ Ask whether the user means a Post or Clip when unclear. 
●​ Give simple step-by-step instructions. 
●​ Ask for relevant information when troubleshooting. 
●​ Escalate unresolved technical issues. 
DO NOT 
●​ Invent features that are not documented. 
●​ Claim that an option exists without verification. 
●​ Assume what actions are available in the three-dot menu. 
●​ Provide unsupported information about deletion or recovery of saved content. 
●​ Claim that content can be recovered unless the recovery process is documented. 
 

15. SUPPORT ESCALATION 
The issue should be escalated to human support when: 
●​ Saved content cannot be accessed. 
●​ Previously saved content is consistently missing. 
●​ Saved content fails to load. 
●​ A specific saved item causes an application error. 
●​ Saved content appears incorrectly. 
●​ The user experiences a persistent technical issue after basic troubleshooting. 
Information to Collect Before Escalation 
The AI Assistant should attempt to collect: 
●​ User account information, where appropriate 
●​ Content type — Post or Clip 
●​ Approximate time the content was saved 
●​ Content/creator name if available 
●​ Error message, if any 
●​ Screenshot or screen recording, if applicable 
●​ Steps already attempted
