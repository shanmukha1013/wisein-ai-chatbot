56. PRIVACY 
Document ID: WI-KB-056​
 Module: Settings​
 Feature: Data Privacy​
 Platform: WiseIN Web Application​
 Document Type: Product Knowledge Base​
 Purpose: RAG Knowledge Base / AI Customer Support​
 Status: ✅ Verified from current UI 
 
1. FEATURE OVERVIEW 
The Data Privacy section allows WiseIN users to understand how their personal data and 
conversations are handled and what control they have over the information they share. 
The page communicates WiseIN's privacy principles and explains that users can manage their 
data through the platform settings. 
Navigation 
Settings → Data Privacy 
 
2. DATA PRIVACY PAGE 
The page is titled: 
Data Privacy 
The main privacy message displayed on the page is: 
Your data, your control 
WiseIN explains that it respects user privacy and states that it collects only the information 
needed to help users connect, communicate, and collaborate with real people. 
 

3. PRIVACY PRINCIPLES 
The Data Privacy page currently presents three primary privacy statements. 
3.1 Data Is Not Sold 
WiseIN states: 
Your data is never sold. 
This communicates that WiseIN does not sell the user's data. 
 
3.2 Conversations Remain Private 
WiseIN states: 
Your conversations stay private. 
This communicates WiseIN's stated privacy approach toward user conversations. 
The AI Assistant should not interpret this statement as permission to expose conversation 
information to other users. 
 
3.3 User Controls Shared Information 
WiseIN states: 
You control what you share and who sees it. 
This indicates that users have control over the information they choose to share and its intended 
visibility. 
 
4. DATA COLLECTION 
The Data Privacy page states that WiseIN: 
collects only what's needed to help you connect, communicate, and collaborate with 
real people. 

The AI Assistant should therefore explain data collection based on WiseIN's documented 
privacy information rather than making assumptions about specific categories of collected data. 
 
5. DATA SECURITY 
The page states that WiseIN uses: 
industry-standard security practices 
to protect user information. 
The AI Assistant may communicate this statement when users ask generally how WiseIN 
protects their information. 
However, the AI should not invent specific security technologies, encryption methods, 
certifications, or compliance standards unless they are documented elsewhere in the WiseIN 
knowledge base. 
 
6. USER DATA CONTROL 
WiseIN states that users can: 
●​ Update their data 
●​ Delete their data 
●​ Manage their data from Settings 
The Data Privacy page specifically states that users can: 
update or delete your data anytime from settings. 
This establishes that users have data-management controls available through the Settings area. 
 
7. PRIVACY POLICY CHANGES 

The Data Privacy page states that if WiseIN changes how data is handled, users will be 
informed: 
If we ever make changes to how data is handled, we'll tell you clearly and 
upfront. 
Therefore, users should be directed to WiseIN's privacy-related information when they have 
questions about changes to data handling. 
 
8. USER PRIVACY QUESTIONS 
Q1. Does WiseIN sell my data? 
AI Response: 
No. WiseIN's Data Privacy page states that your data is never sold. 
 
Q2. Are my conversations private? 
AI Response: 
WiseIN's Data Privacy page states that your conversations stay private. 
The AI should not provide additional technical guarantees beyond what is documented. 
 
Q3. Who can see the information I share? 
AI Response: 
WiseIN states that you control what you share and who sees it. The visibility of 
specific information can depend on the feature and sharing settings associated with 
it. 
 
Q4. Can I update my data? 

AI Response: 
Yes. WiseIN states that you can update your data from Settings. 
 
Q5. Can I delete my data? 
AI Response: 
WiseIN states that you can delete your data from Settings. If you're referring 
specifically to deleting your entire WiseIN account, you can use the Delete 
Account option in Settings. 
 
Q6. How does WiseIN protect my data? 
AI Response: 
WiseIN states that it uses industry-standard security practices to protect your 
information. 
 
Q7. What data does WiseIN collect? 
AI Response: 
WiseIN states that it collects only what's needed to help users connect, 
communicate, and collaborate. For specific information about what data is 
collected, please refer to the relevant WiseIN privacy documentation. 
The AI should not invent a detailed list of collected data unless that information is documented 
elsewhere. 
 
Q8. What happens if WiseIN changes its data practices? 
AI Response: 

WiseIN states that if it makes changes to how data is handled, it will communicate 
those changes clearly and upfront. 
 
9. PRIVACY-RELATED USER ACTIONS 
The Data Privacy page itself primarily provides information rather than presenting multiple 
configuration controls. 
For broader account/data management, users can access relevant options from: 
Settings 
Possible related controls include: 
●​ Data Privacy 
●​ Edit Profile 
●​ Sign In & Security 
●​ Blocked 
●​ Deactivate 
●​ Delete Account 
The exact relationship between each control and personal-data management should be 
documented in its respective knowledge-base module. 
 
10. PRIVACY AND ACCOUNT DELETION 
Privacy-related requests may overlap with account deletion. 
If a user says: 
"I want all my information removed." 
The AI should distinguish between: 
Data Management 
Updating or deleting available user data through Settings. 
Account Deletion 

Permanently deleting the user's WiseIN account through the Delete Account functionality. 
The AI should direct the user to the appropriate documented process rather than assuming that 
every privacy request requires account deletion. 
 
11. PRIVACY AND ACCOUNT 
DEACTIVATION 
Deactivation and deletion should not be treated as the same action. 
If the user asks: 
"Can I temporarily stop using my account?" 
The AI should refer to the Account Deactivation documentation. 
If the user asks: 
"I want to permanently remove my account." 
The AI should refer to the Account Deletion documentation. 
 
12. AI CUSTOMER SUPPORT RULES 
The AI SHOULD 
●​ Explain WiseIN's documented privacy statements. 
●​ Tell users where Data Privacy can be accessed. 
●​ Explain that WiseIN states user data is not sold. 
●​ Explain WiseIN's stated approach to conversation privacy. 
●​ Explain that users control what they share and who sees it. 
●​ Explain that users can update or delete data through Settings. 
●​ Refer users to appropriate privacy/account-management documentation. 
●​ Escalate questions that require information not available in the knowledge base. 
The AI SHOULD NOT 

●​ Invent WiseIN's data collection practices. 
●​ Claim specific encryption technologies without documentation. 
●​ Claim specific legal/compliance certifications without documentation. 
●​ Reveal private user conversations. 
●​ Reveal another user's personal information. 
●​ Claim that all information is permanently deleted immediately without verification. 
●​ Interpret "conversations stay private" beyond the documented statement. 
●​ Make unsupported claims about third-party data sharing. 
 
13. PRIVACY & AI ASSISTANT 
Because WiseIN is being documented for an AI Customer Support Assistant, privacy is 
particularly important. 
The AI Assistant should follow these principles: 
Data Minimization 
Only request information necessary to resolve the user's support issue. 
No Sensitive Credentials 
The assistant should never request: 
●​ Passwords 
●​ OTPs 
●​ UPI PINs 
●​ Card numbers 
●​ CVV 
●​ Authentication credentials 
Conversation Privacy 
The assistant should not expose information from another user's conversation or account. 
Controlled Disclosure 
The assistant should provide only information that the current user is authorized to access. 
 

14. PRIVACY ESCALATION 
A privacy-related question should be escalated when: 
●​ The user reports unauthorized access to their information. 
●​ The user believes another person accessed their account. 
●​ The user reports that private information was exposed. 
●​ The user requests information that the AI is not authorized to provide. 
●​ The user reports a suspected privacy or data-security incident. 
●​ The question requires access to internal privacy records. 
●​ The user requests a data action that cannot be completed through the available 
interface.
