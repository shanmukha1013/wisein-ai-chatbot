3. What is WiseIN? 
WiseIN is a professional networking platform designed to help individuals 
and organizations build meaningful professional connections, discover 
opportunities, communicate, collaborate, and grow within a professional 
ecosystem. 
The WiseIN application brings together professional profiles, networking, 
content discovery, job opportunities, communication, meetings, events, 
conversations, organizations, and analytics in a single platform. 
WiseIN enables users to establish and present their professional identity, 
discover relevant people and organizations, build professional 

relationships, participate in professional discussions, explore opportunities, 
and engage in various forms of professional interaction. 
Organizations can use WiseIN to establish their presence, connect with 
professionals, manage organizational activities, and participate in the 
professional ecosystem. 
3.1 Core Purpose of WiseIN 
WiseIN is designed to provide a centralized platform for professional 
networking and engagement by connecting: 
People with relevant professionals and opportunities. 
Professionals with organizations and career opportunities. 
Organizations with professionals and potential talent. 
Users with professional conversations, events, meetings, and communities. 
Individuals and organizations with tools for professional communication and 
engagement.

4. WiseIN's Purpose 
WiseIN is designed to provide a centralized professional networking and 
engagement platform where individuals and organizations can build 

professional relationships, discover opportunities, communicate, 
collaborate, and participate in professional activities. 
The platform brings multiple aspects of professional networking into one 
ecosystem, reducing the need for users to rely on separate platforms for 
professional identity, networking, communication, opportunities, meetings, 
and professional interactions. 
4.1 Problems WiseIN Addresses 
WiseIN is designed to address several common challenges in professional 
networking and career development: 
1. Difficulty in Finding Relevant Professional Connections 
Professionals may find it difficult to discover people who are relevant to 
their career, interests, skills, or professional goals. 
WiseIN provides tools for discovering professionals, organizations, and 
relevant areas of interest. 
2. Limited Opportunities for Meaningful Professional Interaction 
Traditional professional networking can often be limited to profile-based 
connections or text-based communication. 
WiseIN provides multiple ways for users to engage with professionals 
through networking, chat, Waves, meetings, events, and professional 
conversations. 
3. Difficulty Discovering Relevant Opportunities 
Users may need to search across multiple platforms to find suitable jobs 
and professional opportunities. 
WiseIN provides job discovery and related professional opportunity 
features within the application. 
4. Fragmented Professional Activities 
Professional networking, communication, meetings, job discovery, events, 
and organizational engagement are often handled through different 
applications. 

WiseIN brings these activities together within a single professional 
ecosystem. 
5. Difficulty Establishing a Professional Identity 
Professionals need a structured way to present their experience, education, 
skills, projects, interests, and other relevant information. 
WiseIN provides professional profiles that allow users to create and 
maintain their professional identity. 
6. Challenges in Connecting Professionals with Organizations 
Organizations need ways to establish their professional presence and 
engage with relevant professionals. 
WiseIN provides organization-related functionality for professional 
engagement, networking, opportunities, and organizational activities.

10. Waves 
Waves is a real-time, audio-based professional networking feature within 
WiseIN. It provides users with a quick and interactive way to join live audio 
conversations, meet other professionals, exchange ideas, and participate in 
discussions without requiring a traditional scheduled meeting. 
A Wave can be understood as a quick audio-room style 
meeting/conversation where multiple participants can join and speak about 
a particular topic. 
The Waves module is designed to make professional conversations more 
immediate and accessible by allowing users to participate in live 
discussions with other professionals. 
10.1 Purpose of Waves 
The primary purpose of Waves is to provide a space for: 
Real-time professional conversations. 
Quick audio interactions. 
Networking with other professionals. 
Sharing ideas and knowledge. 
Discussing specific professional topics. 
Discovering new people and conversations. 
Participating in informal professional discussions. 

Building relationships through live interaction. 
Waves are intended for conversation and professional interaction rather 
than functioning as a traditional scheduled meeting.

24.6 Troubleshooting Process

The WiseIN AI Customer Support System should follow a structured 
troubleshooting process.

Determine:

What the user was attempting to do. 
What the user expected to happen. 
What actually happened. 
Whether an error message was displayed. 
Step 2: Collect Relevant Context 
Request only necessary information, such as: 
Feature or page name. 
Steps to reproduce the issue. 

Error message or visible behavior. 
Device and platform information, where relevant. 
Whether the problem is temporary, recurring, or persistent. 
Step 3: Check Approved Documentation 
Search the approved WiseIN knowledge base for: 
Known issues. 
Documented error messages. 
Feature-specific troubleshooting steps. 
Platform-specific instructions. 
Relevant account or permission requirements. 
Step 4: Provide Verified Guidance 
Give clear, ordered troubleshooting instructions based on approved 
documentation. 
The AI should avoid suggesting unverified workarounds that could affect 
user accounts, personal data, payments, security, or other important 
functionality. 
Step 5: Confirm the Result 
Ask whether the suggested steps resolved the issue, where appropriate. 
If the issue is not resolved, the AI should: 
Review whether additional documented steps are available. 
Avoid repeatedly providing the same unsuccessful instructions. 
Offer escalation to human support when required.

26.4 User Journey Guidance for AI Customer Support 
The WiseIN AI Customer Support System should identify the user's current 
stage and objective before providing assistance. 
The AI should: 
Determine whether the user is attempting to register, verify, navigate, use a 
feature, or manage an account. 
Identify the relevant WiseIN module. 
Provide instructions based on verified documentation. 

Ask for relevant clarification when the user's request is ambiguous. 
Avoid assuming that all users have identical permissions or feature access. 
Explain documented errors and troubleshooting steps when applicable. 
Escalate unresolved, account-specific, or undocumented issues to human 
support. 
The AI should not assume that a user has completed verification, has an 
active subscription, or has access to a particular feature without verified 
account information or an approved backend tool. 
26.5 User Journey Completion Criteria 
A user's journey should be considered complete only in relation to the 
specific task or objective they intended to accomplish. 
For example: 
A registration journey is complete when the account creation process 
reaches its documented successful state. 
A profile completion journey is complete when the relevant profile 
information has been saved successfully. 
A job application journey is complete when the application reaches the 
documented submission state. 
A support journey is complete when the user's issue is resolved or 
successfully escalated to human support. 
An account deletion journey is complete only after the documented deletion 
process has been confirmed. 
The AI must not claim that an action has been completed unless the 
application or an authorized backend system provides confirmation.
